## Scope

This slice consumes two already-established authorities rather than inventing a disconnected story:

1. B1 `Free Worlds Relief Cooperative Archive`: emergency shipping networks evolved shared stock lists, trusted carriers, standardized requests, and cooperative payment arrangements.
2. A1 `world: free worlds relief demand`: a persistent, decaying Free Worlds relief-pressure signal generated by emergency arrivals from pirate-held space.

B2 adds two named characters:

- **Lysa Kern** — Free Worlds relief coordinator focused on preserving emergency access even when receiving worlds cannot immediately guarantee payment.
- **Oren Vale** — independent carrier focused on preventing emergency freight from quietly converting carriers into involuntary lenders.

The first conversation offers only while authoritative A1 relief demand is high (`>= 3`) and supports three persistent routes plus refusal:

- standing cooperative reserve;
- guaranteed carrier operating-payment floor;
- split-risk recovery ledger;
- refusal/non-intervention.

The review offers only after that same A1 relief signal has eased (`< 3`). It remembers the earlier B2 route, reflects its consequences, and resolves to one of two persistent institutional settlements:

- **shared recovery ledger** — cooperative reserve pays immediate operating costs; receiving-world recovery obligations are accounted for later and can be adjusted politically after the crisis;
- **mutual relief reserve** — catastrophic relief freight becomes a genuinely shared member-world cost rather than recreating the emergency as debt for the damaged world.

`Vale Remembers` is a later one-shot reader of either terminal settlement.

## B3 continuity notes

The settlement is intentionally framed as institutional procedure rather than a constitutional change to the Free Worlds. Future continuity work should preserve the distinction between:

- local-world fiscal autonomy;
- cooperative emergency freight guarantees;
- independent-carrier commercial risk;
- post-crisis political decisions about repayment/forgiveness.

Avoid converting either settlement into a claim that the Free Worlds have a centralized treasury with unlimited authority unless later canon explicitly establishes that.
