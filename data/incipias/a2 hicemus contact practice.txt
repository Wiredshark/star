mission "A2 Hicemus Contact Practice: Review"
	minor
	invisible
	offer precedence 9
	source
		government "Hicemus"
	to offer
		has "Incipias: Help The Stranded 2: done"
		not "A2 Hicemus Contact Practice: decided"
	on offer
		conversation
			`An Incipias attendant has assembled several entries from the Hicemus contact testimony register: a rescue gesture mistaken for a warning, a color sequence whose meaning changed after later encounters, and a docking instruction that only became clear when the witness sketch was compared with the ship's actual position.`
			`\tThe attendant stresses that these are records of modern contact practice, not a claim that outsiders have decoded a complete Hicemus language. What mattered was the habit of keeping observation, interpretation, and later correction separate.`
			`\tYou are asked what lesson you would carry into your own future encounters.`
			choice
				`Record observations before assigning meaning.`
					`You decide that future contact notes should preserve what was actually seen or heard before attaching an interpretation. A later translation can revise the meaning without rewriting the observation.`
					set "A2 Hicemus Contact Practice: observation first"
					goto done
				`Treat every interpretation as revisable when new evidence arrives.`
					`You decide to carry forward an explicit revision rule: a translation or inferred intent remains provisional when later contact supplies better evidence.`
					set "A2 Hicemus Contact Practice: revision first"
					goto done
				`Keep this lesson local to the Hicemus contact record.`
					`You decide that the archive is useful context without turning one difficult contact history into a universal rule for every species.`
					set "A2 Hicemus Contact Practice: local only"
					goto done
				`Do not adopt a standing practice from this archive.`
					`You decline to turn the Hicemus record into a rule for your own future encounters.`
					set "A2 Hicemus Contact Practice: refused"
					goto done
			done
			`The attendant records none of this as Hicemus policy or endorsement. It is your own handling of evidence from a contact history whose language barrier still matters.`
			set "A2 Hicemus Contact Practice: decided"
			decline


mission "A2 Hicemus Contact Practice: Reflection"
	minor
	invisible
	offer precedence 9
	source
		government "Hicemus"
	to offer
		has "Incipias: Help The Stranded 2: done"
		has "A2 Hicemus Contact Practice: decided"
		not "A2 Hicemus Contact Practice: refused"
		not "A2 Hicemus Contact Practice: reflection seen"
	on offer
		conversation
			branch observation
				has "A2 Hicemus Contact Practice: observation first"
			branch revision
				has "A2 Hicemus Contact Practice: revision first"
			branch local
				has "A2 Hicemus Contact Practice: local only"
			observation
			`A new testimony entry preserves a crew's observed light pattern separately from the meaning first assigned to it. When another witness offers a different interpretation, the underlying observation remains intact instead of being quietly rewritten.`
			goto finish
			revision
			`A translated warning in the register now carries a visible amendment. The earlier interpretation is retained, but a later encounter has narrowed what the archivists think the signal meant. The correction is treated as evidence of a functioning record, not an embarrassment to erase.`
			goto finish
			local
			`The attendant points out a case where a method useful in Hicemus contact would have been misleading elsewhere. Keeping the lesson local preserved the archive's value without pretending one species' communication problem supplied a universal doctrine.`
			finish
			`Nothing in the exchange grants you a Hicemus office, linguistic credential, endorsement, or authority to speak for their government.`
			set "A2 Hicemus Contact Practice: reflection seen"
			decline
