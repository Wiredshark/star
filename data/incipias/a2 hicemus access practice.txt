# A2 Hicemus Access Practice. Consumes B2 settlement state read-only.

mission "A2 Hicemus Access Practice: Reflection"
	landing
	minor
	invisible
	source
		government "Hicemus"
	to offer
		has "B2 Hicemus Access Compact: aftermath seen"
		not "A2 Hicemus Access Practice: chosen"
		random < 24
	on offer
		conversation
			branch conflict
				has "B2 Hicemus Access Compact: settlement shared conflict table"
			`The temporary-route records you helped shape are now ordinary enough that nobody pauses over them. The Incipias you privately call the Dispatcher shows you a copied route notice: purpose, capacity, emergency priority, expiry, and reviewer all travel with the exception. What matters now is whether you treat that lesson as a rule for your own future decisions or as something that belongs only to this station.`
			goto choose
			label conflict
			`The shared conflict table is now ordinary enough that operators consult it before temporary routes overlap. The Incipias you privately call the Maintainer shows you two harmless-looking exceptions whose combined load would have crossed a junction limit. What matters now is whether you carry that habit of checking interactions into your own future decisions or leave it here.`
			label choose
			choice
				`Carry forward a bounded-record practice: temporary exceptions should remember purpose, limits, expiry, and who must review them.`
					action
						"A2 Hicemus Access Practice: chosen" = 1
						"A2 Hicemus Access Practice: bounded record" = 1
					accept
				`Carry forward an interaction-first practice: individually reasonable exceptions must be checked for combined loads and shared emergency paths.`
					action
						"A2 Hicemus Access Practice: chosen" = 1
						"A2 Hicemus Access Practice: interaction first" = 1
					accept
				`Keep the lesson local. This station's access compromises are not a universal rule.`
					action
						"A2 Hicemus Access Practice: chosen" = 1
						"A2 Hicemus Access Practice: local only" = 1
					accept
				`Refuse to turn your participation into a standing practice.`
					action
						"A2 Hicemus Access Practice: chosen" = 1
						"A2 Hicemus Access Practice: refused" = 1
					accept

mission "A2 Hicemus Access Practice: Later Reflection"
	landing
	minor
	invisible
	to offer
		has "A2 Hicemus Access Practice: chosen"
		not "A2 Hicemus Access Practice: reflection seen"
		random < 30
	on offer
		conversation
			branch interaction
				has "A2 Hicemus Access Practice: interaction first"
			branch local
				has "A2 Hicemus Access Practice: local only"
			branch refused
				has "A2 Hicemus Access Practice: refused"
			`A temporary operating exception crosses your desk. Because you kept the Hicemus lesson as a bounded-record practice, you notice that its purpose is clear but its expiry and reviewer are not. You send it back for those limits before treating the exception as permanent. The useful part of the lesson traveled without pretending Hicemus practice is universal.`
			goto done
			label interaction
			`Two individually sensible temporary arrangements overlap in a way neither request mentions. Because you kept the interaction-first lesson, you compare their combined burden before accepting either at face value. The habit travels; the station's internal authority does not.`
			goto done
			label local
			`A later access dispute reminds you of the Hicemus station, but the people, risks, and institutions are different. You deliberately resist importing its settlement as precedent. Remembering where an analogy stops proves as useful as remembering the analogy itself.`
			goto done
			label refused
			`A later access dispute reminds you of the Hicemus station. You remember choosing not to turn that episode into a standing rule and judge the new case on its own evidence. Participation in one settlement did not appoint you to carry its authority elsewhere.`
			label done
			action
				"A2 Hicemus Access Practice: reflection seen" = 1
			accept
