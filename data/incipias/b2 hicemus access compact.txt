# B2 Hicemus Access Compact production slice.
# Consumes the B1 Hicemus Station Access Archive after first formal human contact.
# The player privately uses Dispatcher and Maintainer as shorthand for two recurring
# Incipias; neither word is a canonical name, title, office, or statement about the
# political meaning of the Hicemus/Conlatio division.

mission "B2 Hicemus Access Compact: Offer"
	landing
	minor
	invisible
	source
		government "Hicemus"
	to offer
		has "Incipias: Help The Stranded 2: done"
		has "Hicemus History: Station Access Archive: offered"
		not "B2 Hicemus Access Compact: introduced"
		not "B2 Hicemus Access Compact: declined"
		random < 18
	on offer
		conversation
			`Near a junction in the Hicemus station, two Incipias are arguing in fast-changing bands of color while a routing display repeatedly redraws the same three corridors. You have seen both before. One is usually redirecting arrivals, cargo carts, and visitors, so in your own head you have begun calling that one the Dispatcher. The other is usually beside access panels and life-support machinery, so you privately think of that one as the Maintainer. Neither word is a name or title they have given you.`
			`\tA damaged transfer lock has closed one of the station's normal freight passages. The Dispatcher wants to move cargo through a quiet residential corridor until repairs are complete. The Maintainer objects that the same corridor is the cleanest backup route for emergency crews reaching a life-support junction, while residents rely on it for a measure of privacy from heavy station traffic. Both can show old routing maps supporting their position at different times in the station's history.`
			`\tThe disagreement is not about whether cargo or emergencies matter. It is about how a crowded orbital settlement should decide which temporary use gets priority when one corridor is carrying several kinds of obligation at once.`
			choice
				`\tProtect the emergency route. Cargo can wait or take the longer path; life-support access should never depend on ordinary traffic remaining light.`
					goto emergency
				`\tUse the residential corridor for freight, but only under a published capacity limit that clears immediately when an emergency call appears.`
					goto conditional
				`\tSeparate priority from ownership: publish a temporary route table with explicit cargo windows, emergency preemption, and a review time before the exception becomes normal.`
					goto compact
				`\tDecline to settle access rules for a station whose internal obligations you only partly understand.`
					goto decline
			label emergency
			`You point first to the life-support junction and then to the clear backup path. The Maintainer's colors settle. Freight is sent on a longer loop through a working cargo passage. The Dispatcher accepts the decision, but shows you the growing queue at the transfer locks: a rule that protects emergency access perfectly can still impose accumulating costs on everyone who depends on ordinary movement.`
			action
				"B2 Hicemus Access Compact: introduced" = 1
				"B2 Hicemus Access Compact: route emergency" = 1
				"B2 Hicemus Access Compact: maintainer trusts player" = 1
			accept
			label conditional
			`You point to the freight queue, mark a limited flow through the quiet corridor, and then mimic the display's emergency symbol overriding everything else. The Dispatcher brightens and programs a capacity limit. The Maintainer adds a hard preemption rule. The immediate queue shrinks, but both now depend on every future operator recognizing when a temporary exception has become too crowded to remain safe.`
			action
				"B2 Hicemus Access Compact: introduced" = 1
				"B2 Hicemus Access Compact: route conditional" = 1
				"B2 Hicemus Access Compact: dispatcher trusts player" = 1
			accept
			label compact
			`You trace three separate marks on the display: a freight window, an emergency override, and a time when the whole arrangement must be reviewed. After several exchanges the Dispatcher and Maintainer add all three. The route remains shared, but the exception now carries its own limits and an expiration point instead of quietly becoming the new default.`
			action
				"B2 Hicemus Access Compact: introduced" = 1
				"B2 Hicemus Access Compact: route compact" = 1
				"B2 Hicemus Access Compact: dispatcher trusts player" = 1
				"B2 Hicemus Access Compact: maintainer trusts player" = 1
			accept
			label decline
			`You hold up both hands and step away from the display. The two Incipias return to their argument, alternating between the old maps and the live traffic count. When you leave, the freight queue is still growing and the backup route remains undecided.`
			action
				"B2 Hicemus Access Compact: declined" = 1
			decline

mission "B2 Hicemus Access Compact: Review"
	landing
	minor
	invisible
	source
		government "Hicemus"
	to offer
		has "B2 Hicemus Access Compact: introduced"
		not "B2 Hicemus Access Compact: reviewed"
		random < 24
	on offer
		conversation
			branch emergency
				has "B2 Hicemus Access Compact: route emergency"
			branch conditional
				has "B2 Hicemus Access Compact: route conditional"
			`The Dispatcher and Maintainer find you beside a station map covered in temporary route markers. Your earlier shared-route compact worked well enough that other departments copied it. Now the station has several exceptions, each individually sensible, whose windows and preemption rules overlap in ways no single display explains.`
			`\tThe problem is no longer one blocked freight passage. Temporary access decisions are becoming a second routing system layered on top of the permanent one. If the station cannot remember why each exception exists, an emergency crew may encounter a corridor that is technically available but practically unusable.`
			choice
				`\tCreate a portable access record: every temporary route carries its purpose, capacity, emergency priority, expiry, and the person responsible for closing or renewing it.`
					goto record
				`\tCreate a conflict table: temporary routes remain local decisions, but overlapping exceptions must be checked against one shared list of emergency paths and maximum simultaneous loads.`
					goto conflict
			label emergency
			`Your earlier emergency-first rule kept the backup path clear, but the Dispatcher shows you several longer freight detours that have now become habitual. None blocks life support, yet together they create congestion around other maintenance corridors. Protecting one critical path solved the immediate danger without recording the new burdens displaced elsewhere.`
			choice
				`\tMake every detour carry a portable access record with purpose, capacity, emergency priority, expiry, and closure responsibility.`
					goto record
				`\tKeep local detours flexible, but require every one to be checked against a shared conflict table for emergency paths and simultaneous loads.`
					goto conflict
			label conditional
			`Your earlier capacity-limited freight exception became popular. The Dispatcher shows you several routes using the same idea. The Maintainer then overlays them and reveals that two individually safe limits can still overload a shared junction when their traffic peaks at the same time. Local limits are not enough when the routes intersect.`
			choice
				`\tMake every exception carry a portable access record that exposes purpose, capacity, priority, expiry, and closure responsibility wherever it is copied.`
					goto record
				`\tKeep local capacity rules, but require a shared conflict table that catches overlapping emergency paths and combined loads before approval.`
					goto conflict
			label record
			`You recommend a portable access record. A temporary route may still be created quickly, but its reason, capacity, emergency-preemption rule, expiry, and responsible reviewer must travel with every copied map or routing instruction. A later operator can tell not only that a corridor was opened, but why, for how long, and who must decide whether the exception still belongs there.`
			action
				"B2 Hicemus Access Compact: reviewed" = 1
				"B2 Hicemus Access Compact: settlement portable access record" = 1
				"B2 Hicemus Access Compact: dispatcher trusts player" = 1
				"B2 Hicemus Access Compact: maintainer trusts player" = 1
			accept
			label conflict
			`You recommend a shared conflict table. Departments may still make local routing decisions, but every temporary exception is checked against the same emergency corridors, junction capacities, and simultaneous-load limits before it activates. The station keeps local discretion while making the interactions between separate exceptions visible before they become a crisis.`
			action
				"B2 Hicemus Access Compact: reviewed" = 1
				"B2 Hicemus Access Compact: settlement shared conflict table" = 1
				"B2 Hicemus Access Compact: dispatcher trusts player" = 1
				"B2 Hicemus Access Compact: maintainer trusts player" = 1
			accept

mission "B2 Hicemus Access Compact: Maintainer Remembers"
	landing
	minor
	invisible
	source
		government "Hicemus"
	to offer
		not "B2 Hicemus Access Compact: aftermath seen"
		or
			has "B2 Hicemus Access Compact: settlement portable access record"
			has "B2 Hicemus Access Compact: settlement shared conflict table"
		random < 28
	on offer
		conversation
			branch conflict
				has "B2 Hicemus Access Compact: settlement shared conflict table"
			`The Maintainer notices you near an access panel and brings up a temporary-route notice. Beside the route itself are its purpose, capacity, emergency priority, expiry, and the reviewer who must close or renew it. The Dispatcher arrives with a copied map from another section; the same record is attached there too. The station has not stopped improvising, but its improvisations now remember why they exist and when they should end.`
			goto done
			label conflict
			`The Maintainer notices you near an access panel and overlays several temporary routes on the station map. Before any of them activate, the display highlights a junction where two harmless local exceptions would exceed a shared load limit. The Dispatcher reroutes one window without argument. The station still lets departments improvise, but the consequences of those separate decisions are visible before they collide.`
			label done
			action
				"B2 Hicemus Access Compact: aftermath seen" = 1
			accept
