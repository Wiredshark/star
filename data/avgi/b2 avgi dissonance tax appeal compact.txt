mission "B2 Avgi Dissonance Tax Appeal Compact: Offer"
	minor
	name `A Complaint That Can Survive Copying`
	description `Dissonance records advocate Indigo and workshop accountant Sienna disagree over how tax challenges should travel between worlds without turning an accusation into a verdict.`
	source
		government "Avgi (Dissonance)"
	to offer
		has "language: Avgi (Written)"
		not "avgi: lost in twilight"
		not "B2 Avgi Dissonance Tax Appeal Compact: introduced"
		not "B2 Avgi Dissonance Tax Appeal Compact: declined"
	on offer
		conversation
			`A public records kiosk has been converted into a temporary appeal desk. Indigo, a Dissonance records advocate, is arranging copied tax notices beside challenge forms. Sienna, an accountant from a nearby cooperative workshop, is objecting to a summary that marks an assessment as disputed without also carrying the later correction.`
			`Indigo taps the original challenge. "The objection must remain visible. A copied record that carries only the final amount can erase why the challenge existed." Sienna answers, "And a copied record that carries only the objection can make a corrected assessment look corrupt forever. We need memory without turning grievance into proof."`
			choice
				`\tBack Indigo: every copied assessment should carry the original objection and its evidence trail.`
					goto indigo
				`\tBack Sienna: downstream records should emphasize the current verified assessment and link back to prior challenges.`
					goto sienna
				`\tPropose a paired record: current assessment, challenge basis, evidence, disposition, and open-or-closed status travel together.`
					goto paired
				`\tDecline to referee a Dissonance tax-record dispute.`
					goto decline
			label indigo
			`You argue that a challenge which disappears from later copies cannot protect anyone from repeated inconsistent treatment. Indigo agrees, but Sienna insists that the record must also show whether the challenge was upheld, corrected, rejected, or still unresolved.`
			action
				"B2 Avgi Dissonance Tax Appeal Compact: introduced" = 1
				"B2 Avgi Dissonance Tax Appeal Compact: route indigo" = 1
				"B2 Avgi Dissonance Tax Appeal Compact: indigo trusts player" = 1
			accept
			label sienna
			`You argue that current operations need a current verified figure, not a permanent warning banner. Sienna approves. Indigo accepts the operational point but warns that the link to the challenge record must remain durable enough that a later dispute can reconstruct how the number changed.`
			action
				"B2 Avgi Dissonance Tax Appeal Compact: introduced" = 1
				"B2 Avgi Dissonance Tax Appeal Compact: route sienna" = 1
				"B2 Avgi Dissonance Tax Appeal Compact: sienna trusts player" = 1
			accept
			label paired
			`You suggest separating the current verified assessment from the history that produced it while making the link mandatory. Each copied record would carry the assessment, the challenge basis, the evidence used, the disposition, and whether anything remains open. Indigo calls it protest memory that cannot impersonate a verdict. Sienna calls it a ledger she can actually use.`
			action
				"B2 Avgi Dissonance Tax Appeal Compact: introduced" = 1
				"B2 Avgi Dissonance Tax Appeal Compact: route paired" = 1
				"B2 Avgi Dissonance Tax Appeal Compact: indigo trusts player" = 1
				"B2 Avgi Dissonance Tax Appeal Compact: sienna trusts player" = 1
			accept
			label decline
			`You tell them that Dissonance communities should decide how their own grievance records travel. Indigo says that refusal is at least better than pretending an outsider's preference is neutral. Sienna returns to the copied assessments.`
			action
				"B2 Avgi Dissonance Tax Appeal Compact: declined" = 1
			decline

mission "B2 Avgi Dissonance Tax Appeal Compact: Review"
	minor
	name `Old Objection, New Copy`
	description `Return after Indigo and Sienna test the tax-appeal record format across several Dissonance offices.`
	source
		government "Avgi (Dissonance)"
	to offer
		has "language: Avgi (Written)"
		not "avgi: lost in twilight"
		has "B2 Avgi Dissonance Tax Appeal Compact: introduced"
		not "B2 Avgi Dissonance Tax Appeal Compact: reviewed"
	on offer
		conversation
			branch indigo
				has "B2 Avgi Dissonance Tax Appeal Compact: route indigo"
			branch sienna
				has "B2 Avgi Dissonance Tax Appeal Compact: route sienna"
			`Indigo and Sienna show you three copies of the same old assessment. The current amount is identical on all three, but one copy has lost the reason the challenge was filed, another has lost the disposition, and the third still marks the challenge as active even though it was closed months ago.`
			choice
				`\tAdopt a portable disposition packet that keeps challenge basis, evidence, current assessment, disposition, and open-or-closed status together.`
					goto packet
				`\tAdopt an expiry-and-renewal rule: resolved challenges stop propagating as active warnings unless new evidence opens a new review.`
					goto expiry
			label indigo
			`Indigo shows how preserving objections without their later disposition caused a workshop to be flagged twice for an issue that had already been corrected. "The grievance survived," Indigo says, "but the answer did not." Sienna points out that the result is politically useful to nobody if every old challenge becomes permanent suspicion.`
			choice
				`\tBind objection, evidence, disposition, and current assessment into one portable packet.`
					goto packet
				`\tClose resolved challenges operationally, but preserve them in history unless fresh evidence reopens review.`
					goto expiry
			label sienna
			`Sienna shows a copied assessment that correctly carries the current amount but only a bare link to an unavailable older archive. Indigo says that a link which cannot survive transfer is not enough. Sienna agrees that the current record needs a compact explanation of why the figure changed, not merely a pointer that may later disappear.`
			choice
				`\tMake the compact explanation and disposition travel with every downstream copy.`
					goto packet
				`\tLet resolved challenges expire as active warnings, while retaining a durable historical record and a clear rule for reopening.`
					goto expiry
			label packet
			`You recommend a portable disposition packet. Every copied assessment carries the challenge basis, evidence actually considered, current verified amount, disposition, and whether any question remains open. Indigo likes that a complaint can survive copying without becoming a verdict. Sienna likes that a resolved complaint cannot masquerade as an active defect.`
			action
				"B2 Avgi Dissonance Tax Appeal Compact: reviewed" = 1
				"B2 Avgi Dissonance Tax Appeal Compact: settlement disposition packet" = 1
				"B2 Avgi Dissonance Tax Appeal Compact: indigo trusts player" = 1
			accept
			label expiry
			`You recommend an expiry-and-renewal rule. A resolved challenge remains in historical records but stops propagating as an active warning. Reopening requires fresh evidence or a newly identified inconsistency, not the mere existence of the old objection. Indigo calls it memory with a burden of renewal. Sienna calls it the difference between history and a permanent accusation.`
			action
				"B2 Avgi Dissonance Tax Appeal Compact: reviewed" = 1
				"B2 Avgi Dissonance Tax Appeal Compact: settlement expiry renewal" = 1
				"B2 Avgi Dissonance Tax Appeal Compact: sienna trusts player" = 1
			accept
