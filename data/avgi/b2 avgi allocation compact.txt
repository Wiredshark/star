mission "B2 Avgi Allocation Compact: Review"
	minor
	name `The Same Reserve Picture`
	description `Return after the Consonance allocation office and Twilight Guard have tested the emergency-reserve arrangement you helped shape.`
	source
		government "Avgi (Consonance)"
	to offer
		has "language: Avgi (Written)"
		not "avgi: lost in twilight"
		has "B2 Avgi Allocation Compact: introduced"
		not "B2 Avgi Allocation Compact: reviewed"
	on offer
		conversation
			branch verdigris
				has "B2 Avgi Allocation Compact: route verdigris"
			branch ochre
				has "B2 Avgi Allocation Compact: route ochre"
			`Verdigris and Ochre have replaced the old side-by-side ledgers with a single emergency reserve board. Civilian-service floors, Guard response classes, and discretionary loads are listed together. Ochre says, "Result. During the last alert, my office drew reserve power without waiting for a second authorization because the approved response class was already visible." Verdigris adds, "And the civilian floor remained visible while they did it."`
			choice
				`\tFormalize the shared board as a public emergency ledger with protected civilian floors and pre-authorized Guard response classes.`
					goto publicledger
				`\tKeep separate authorities, but require a dual-threshold release with automatic joint review when a draw crosses declared limits.`
					goto dualthreshold
			label verdigris
			`Verdigris shows you a test report from a simulated grid failure. The civilian floor prevented an industrial reserve transfer from consuming power assigned to shelters and medical staging, but Ochre's office had to request an exception when interceptor charging exceeded the preplanned defense allotment. Ochre says, "Criticism. The exception arrived in time because this was a drill." Verdigris replies, "Then the threshold needs a faster exception path, not an invisible one."`
			choice
				`\tPublish one emergency ledger with protected civilian floors and pre-authorized Guard response classes.`
					goto publicledger
				`\tUse a dual-threshold release so exceptional Guard draws can happen immediately but trigger mandatory joint review.`
					goto dualthreshold
			label ochre
			`Ochre shows you a real alert log. Their office drew reserve energy immediately for warning relays and interceptor readiness, then reported the draw after the alert. Verdigris found that the action had displaced a scheduled industrial load that nobody had marked as deferrable. "Complaint," Verdigris says. "The Guard moved quickly because the ledger knew only what the Guard needed." Ochre answers, "Agreement. Then teach the ledger what everyone can afford to lose."`
			choice
				`\tMove to one public emergency ledger so every protected and deferrable load is visible before the alarm.`
					goto publicledger
				`\tPreserve immediate Guard authority, but make any draw beyond declared thresholds trigger automatic joint review and restoration planning.`
					goto dualthreshold
			label publicledger
			`You recommend a public emergency ledger shared by the Consonance allocation office and Twilight Guard. Civilian-service floors are published in advance, Guard response classes receive explicit pre-authorization, discretionary loads are marked as deferrable, and every emergency draw is recorded against the same reserve picture. Verdigris calls it allocation authority that can be audited before and after a crisis. Ochre calls it a battle plan for electricity.`
			action
				"B2 Avgi Allocation Compact: reviewed" = 1
				"B2 Avgi Allocation Compact: settlement public emergency ledger" = 1
				"B2 Avgi Allocation Compact: verdigris trusts player" = 1
			accept
			label dualthreshold
			`You recommend preserving separate civilian and Guard authority while defining explicit release thresholds in advance. The Guard may draw immediately inside its declared emergency class. A draw beyond that class is still permitted when delay would be dangerous, but it automatically creates a joint review record identifying what was displaced, why the threshold was crossed, and what must be restored first afterward. Ochre accepts the speed. Verdigris accepts that exceptional authority now leaves an exceptional record.`
			action
				"B2 Avgi Allocation Compact: reviewed" = 1
				"B2 Avgi Allocation Compact: settlement dual threshold" = 1
				"B2 Avgi Allocation Compact: ochre trusts player" = 1
			accept
