			`Near the spaceport's emergency shelters, a wall is covered with faded diagrams showing evacuation routes, radiation warnings, interceptor launch orders, and lists of civilian supply caches. A plaque explains that the Twilight Guard's modern procedures were assembled piecemeal during repeated attacks, when settlements could not rely on intact orbital infrastructure or timely reinforcement.`
			`	The oldest instructions are almost improvisational; later versions standardize shelter signals, reserve crews, medical staging points, and the handoff of local authority when communications fail. The exhibit presents this accumulated routine, rather than any single battle, as the reason some Avgi communities endured long enough to rebuild.`

			`An illuminated route table hangs beside a bank of navigational terminals. Its oldest entries are hand-drawn chains of systems, corrected repeatedly where lanes shifted or instruments became unreliable. The accompanying text says that pilots crossing the tangled shroud once passed safe-route knowledge directly from crew to crew, until the Twilight Guard began collecting reports into a shared register.`
			`	The register never made the region predictable, but it turned isolated experience into institutional memory: uncertain routes could be compared, warnings propagated, and escort crews briefed from something more durable than rumor. Modern charts still mark many entries as provisional.`
