			`\tThe exhibit describes these exchanges as both farm infrastructure and ecological insurance. Settlements share productive varieties, but they also preserve older strains and associated species so that a blight, failed harvest, or overused field does not force every world toward the same fragile solution. The register became a record of what could be shared without making every ecosystem identical.`
