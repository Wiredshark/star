# B2 Deep Veteran Shadow Compact.
# A younger Deep escort pilot has started copying a veteran captain's choices as
# though reputation were a substitute for judgment. This is one mentoring
# relationship, not a Deep command doctrine or Pilot Guild rule.


event "B2 Deep Veteran Shadow Compact: Review Ready"
	"B2 Deep Veteran Shadow Compact: review ready" = 1


mission "B2 Deep Veteran Shadow Compact: Offer"
	minor
	name `A Good Example Is Not a Flight Plan`
	description `A young Deep escort pilot asks what it means to learn from a veteran captain without turning imitation into judgment.`
	source
		government "Deep"
		not attributes "station"
	"offer precedence" 8
	to offer
		"combat rating" >= 80
		not "B2 Deep Veteran Shadow Compact: introduced"
		not "B2 Deep Veteran Shadow Compact: declined"
	on offer
		conversation
			`Eva Pell, an older Deep mechanic, catches your sleeve near a departure board. Beside her is Lio Marr, a young escort pilot who looks embarrassed to have been brought over. Eva says, "He has watched too many recordings of captains who survived long enough to become stories."`
			`Lio folds his arms. "I study people who know what they are doing. Your combat record is not rumor. But everyone keeps telling me I am copying the result instead of learning the reason."`
			choice
				`\tStudy the decision, not the signature move. The same maneuver can be wise once and reckless the next time.`
					goto reasoning
				`\tA veteran can be a useful example, but disagreement is part of learning. You still own the decision when you are in the cockpit.`
					goto judgment
				`\tKeep paired notes: what an experienced captain actually did, and your current assessment of why it does or does not fit this situation.`
					goto paired
				`\tI am not interested in becoming somebody else's doctrine.`
					goto decline
			label reasoning
			`You tell Lio that competence is not a collection of reusable moves. The useful part of a veteran example is the problem, the information available, the risk accepted, and the options that were deliberately left open.`
			action
				"B2 Deep Veteran Shadow Compact: introduced" = 1
				"B2 Deep Veteran Shadow Compact: route decision reasoning" = 1
				"B2 Deep Veteran Shadow Compact: Lio trusts player" = 1
				event "B2 Deep Veteran Shadow Compact: Review Ready" 7 11
				decline
			label judgment
			`You tell Lio that mentorship becomes dangerous when admiration erases disagreement. A useful example should make his judgment sharper, not replace it.`
			action
				"B2 Deep Veteran Shadow Compact: introduced" = 1
				"B2 Deep Veteran Shadow Compact: route independent judgment" = 1
				"B2 Deep Veteran Shadow Compact: Eva trusts player" = 1
				event "B2 Deep Veteran Shadow Compact: Review Ready" 7 11
				decline
			label paired
			`You suggest two columns whenever Lio studies a veteran action: what was actually observed, and what Lio currently thinks it means for his own flight. The first can remain history even when the second changes.`
			action
				"B2 Deep Veteran Shadow Compact: introduced" = 1
				"B2 Deep Veteran Shadow Compact: route paired example assessment" = 1
				"B2 Deep Veteran Shadow Compact: Lio trusts player" = 1
				"B2 Deep Veteran Shadow Compact: Eva trusts player" = 1
				event "B2 Deep Veteran Shadow Compact: Review Ready" 7 11
				decline
			label decline
			`You tell them that your combat record is public enough already and that you do not want to turn one conversation into a mentoring rule.`
			action
				"B2 Deep Veteran Shadow Compact: declined" = 1
				decline


mission "B2 Deep Veteran Shadow Compact: Review"
	minor
	name `The Legend Got Bigger`
	description `Lio's first independent escort decision is being judged against a veteran reputation that may have grown since your first conversation.`
	source
		government "Deep"
		not attributes "station"
	"offer precedence" 8
	to offer
		has "B2 Deep Veteran Shadow Compact: introduced"
		has "B2 Deep Veteran Shadow Compact: review ready"
		not "B2 Deep Veteran Shadow Compact: reviewed"
	on offer
		conversation
			branch famous
				"combat rating" >= 160
			branch judgment
				has "B2 Deep Veteran Shadow Compact: route independent judgment"
			branch paired
				has "B2 Deep Veteran Shadow Compact: route paired example assessment"
			`Eva finds you first. Lio recently broke formation to shepherd a damaged freighter away from a fight rather than chase a retreating attacker. The escort succeeded, but another pilot called the choice timid because it did not look like the stories people tell about veteran captains.`
				goto settle
			label famous
			`Your reputation has grown since you first spoke with Lio. Eva says that has made the problem worse: every clean victory becomes another simplified story about what a "real veteran" would do. Lio's quiet rescue choice is now being compared to a legend rather than a decision.`
				goto settle
			label judgment
			`Lio says the disagreement lesson helped. He ignored another pilot's imitation of a famous attack pattern and chose the escort objective instead. "I still learned from the veterans," he says. "I just stopped pretending they were in my cockpit."`
				goto settle
			label paired
			`Lio shows you his paired notes. The observed examples are unchanged; his assessments have crossed-out assumptions and new annotations. One copied training summary, however, flattened both columns back into a list of veteran-approved maneuvers.`
			label settle
			choice
				`\tUse an independent-practice record: examples remain attributable history, but each pilot records the present objective, evidence, alternatives, and their own decision.`
					goto practice
				`\tUse explicit mentor attribution: advice can remain credited to a veteran, but only the advice actually given travels; current choices remain the learner's responsibility.`
					goto attribution
			label practice
			`You make the learner's reasoning visible without converting it into another rigid checklist. A reader can compare Lio's decision with veteran examples without claiming that similarity proves wisdom or difference proves failure.`
			action
				"B2 Deep Veteran Shadow Compact: reviewed" = 1
				"B2 Deep Veteran Shadow Compact: settlement independent practice" = 1
				"B2 Deep Veteran Shadow Compact: Lio trusts player" = 1
				decline
			label attribution
			`You keep mentor influence attributable and narrow. What you actually told Lio can remain part of his history; later pilots cannot expand that into blanket approval of choices you never reviewed.`
			action
				"B2 Deep Veteran Shadow Compact: reviewed" = 1
				"B2 Deep Veteran Shadow Compact: settlement explicit mentor attribution" = 1
				"B2 Deep Veteran Shadow Compact: Eva trusts player" = 1
				decline


mission "B2 Deep Veteran Shadow Compact: Lio Remembers"
	minor
	name `Lio Remembers`
	description `Lio remembers whether learning from a veteran became a method for thinking or a shortcut around his own judgment.`
	source
		government "Deep"
		not attributes "station"
	"offer precedence" 8
	to offer
		not "B2 Deep Veteran Shadow Compact: aftermath seen"
		or
			has "B2 Deep Veteran Shadow Compact: settlement independent practice"
			has "B2 Deep Veteran Shadow Compact: settlement explicit mentor attribution"
	on offer
		conversation
			branch attribution
				has "B2 Deep Veteran Shadow Compact: settlement explicit mentor attribution"
			`Lio tells you he recently disagreed with one of your old recorded choices while training a new escort pilot. He looks almost pleased about it. "That was the point, right? If I can explain why my situation is different, I am finally learning instead of tracing."`
				goto finish
			label attribution
			`Lio says a new pilot asked what you would have done in a recent escort dispute. "I told them what you actually taught me, then I told them I had no idea what you would choose now. Those are different facts."`
			label finish
			action
				"B2 Deep Veteran Shadow Compact: aftermath seen" = 1
				decline
