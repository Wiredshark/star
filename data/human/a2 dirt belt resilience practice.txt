mission "A2 Dirt Belt Resilience Practice"
	minor
	source
		government "Republic"
		attributes "dirt belt"
		not attributes "station"
	"offer precedence" 9
	to offer
		random < 1
		not "A2 Dirt Belt Resilience Practice: decided"
	on offer
		conversation
			`A cooperative clerk has laid four old records side by side: a water-share ledger, a machinery pool register, a drought-routing manifest, and a mutual-maintenance book. None describes abundance. Each describes a way to keep scarcity from becoming invisible until the next emergency.`
			`\tThe clerk asks what lesson you would actually carry away from them. "Not what the Dirt Belt commands anyone else to do. Just what you would remember when another shortage turns up somewhere different."`
			choice
				`Keep obligations visible: emergency help should record what was borrowed, what remains owed, and what capacity is actually available.`
					goto obligations
				`Track usable capacity, not merely promised supply: delivery is incomplete until people can receive, move, store, and use it.`
					goto capacity
				`Preserve redundancy: shared seed, machinery, specialists, and parts are valuable because one failure need not erase the whole productive base.`
					goto redundancy
				`Keep the Dirt Belt lesson local. These arrangements grew from particular settlements and shortages.`
					goto local
			label obligations
			`You decide that mutual aid stays honest when the record includes unfinished obligations as carefully as successful rescues. The clerk nods. "A favor can be generous and still have a cost worth remembering."`
			action
				"A2 Dirt Belt Resilience Practice: decided" = 1
				"A2 Dirt Belt Resilience Practice: obligations" = 1
				"A2 Dirt Belt Resilience Practice: reflection pending" = 1
				decline
			label capacity
			`You decide that logistics should be judged by usable capacity rather than the moment cargo crosses a port boundary. The clerk taps the old routing ledger. "That distinction saved more harvests than the manifest totals ever showed."`
			action
				"A2 Dirt Belt Resilience Practice: decided" = 1
				"A2 Dirt Belt Resilience Practice: capacity" = 1
				"A2 Dirt Belt Resilience Practice: reflection pending" = 1
				decline
			label redundancy
			`You decide that resilience comes from preserving alternatives before a single failure becomes catastrophic. The clerk gestures toward the cooperative inventories. "Redundancy looks wasteful right up until the only pump breaks."`
			action
				"A2 Dirt Belt Resilience Practice: decided" = 1
				"A2 Dirt Belt Resilience Practice: redundancy" = 1
				"A2 Dirt Belt Resilience Practice: reflection pending" = 1
				decline
			label local
			`You decline to turn local history into a portable doctrine. The clerk accepts that without argument. "Context is part of the record too."`
			action
				"A2 Dirt Belt Resilience Practice: decided" = 1
				"A2 Dirt Belt Resilience Practice: local" = 1
				"A2 Dirt Belt Resilience Practice: reflection pending" = 1
				decline


mission "A2 Dirt Belt Resilience Practice Reflection"
	minor
	source
		government "Republic"
		attributes "dirt belt"
		not attributes "station"
	"offer precedence" 9
	to offer
		has "A2 Dirt Belt Resilience Practice: reflection pending"
		not "A2 Dirt Belt Resilience Practice: reflection seen"
	on offer
		conversation
			branch obligations
				has "A2 Dirt Belt Resilience Practice: obligations"
			branch capacity
				has "A2 Dirt Belt Resilience Practice: capacity"
			branch redundancy
				has "A2 Dirt Belt Resilience Practice: redundancy"
			branch local
				has "A2 Dirt Belt Resilience Practice: local"
			label obligations
			`The clerk shows you a repair exchange whose emergency work is complete but whose replacement-stock obligation is still open. Because the debt stayed visible, the next request begins with a real capacity picture instead of a myth that the previous help was free.`
				goto finish
			label capacity
			`A relief shipment arrived on schedule, but the receiving settlement lost warehouse space after a pump failure. The routing board changes destination before the cargo becomes a successful delivery on paper and a useless one on the ground.`
				goto finish
			label redundancy
			`A cooperative harvester fails during a narrow planting window. The reserve machine is inefficient and expensive to move, but it exists. The clerk notes that redundancy bought time rather than abundance, exactly the distinction you chose to remember.`
				goto finish
			label local
			`The clerk remembers that you kept the lesson local. A current shortage review cites the old records only as local history, not as a rule for other settlements. "Context traveled farther because nobody pretended authority traveled with it."`
			label finish
			action
				"A2 Dirt Belt Resilience Practice: reflection pending" = 0
				"A2 Dirt Belt Resilience Practice: reflection seen" = 1
				decline
