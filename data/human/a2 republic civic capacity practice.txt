mission "A2 Republic Civic Capacity Practice"
	minor
	source
		government "Republic"
	destination "Earth"
	"offer precedence" 8
	to offer
		"world: republic civic strain" >= 2
		not "A2 Republic Civic Capacity Practice: decided"
	on offer
		conversation
			`Administrator Jo Ren has three queue reports open at once: temporary housing, travel documentation, and secondary review. "The same families keep crossing all three desks. When the system is strained, a local optimization can simply move the wait somewhere else."`
			`\tRen asks what operating rule you would preserve while the combined civic workload remains elevated.`
			choice
				`Preserve case continuity: one visible record should follow a person across offices so repeated intake does not manufacture new delay.`
					goto continuity
				`Publish capacity and queue age: route cases according to real staffing and delay instead of nominal office boundaries.`
					goto capacity
				`Protect urgent exceptions, but require each exception to leave a reason and a later review trail.`
					goto exceptions
				`Refuse to establish a standing rule from one overloaded period.`
					goto refuse
			label continuity
			`You choose continuity. Ren marks the principle beside the overlapping case lists. "Then transfer is not a restart. The next desk inherits the record and the time already spent waiting."`
			action
				"A2 Republic Civic Capacity Practice: decided" = 1
				"A2 Republic Civic Capacity Practice: continuity" = 1
				decline
			label capacity
			`You choose transparent capacity. Ren nods. "A shorter queue is only useful if the office can actually finish the work."`
			action
				"A2 Republic Civic Capacity Practice: decided" = 1
				"A2 Republic Civic Capacity Practice: capacity" = 1
				decline
			label exceptions
			`You protect urgent exceptions but insist that urgency remain reviewable. Ren writes down reason, scope, and review as separate fields. "Emergency access without invisible privilege."`
			action
				"A2 Republic Civic Capacity Practice: decided" = 1
				"A2 Republic Civic Capacity Practice: exceptions" = 1
				decline
			label refuse
			`You decline to turn an emergency workload into standing administrative doctrine. Ren accepts the boundary. "Then the overload stays evidence, not authority."`
			action
				"A2 Republic Civic Capacity Practice: decided" = 1
				"A2 Republic Civic Capacity Practice: refused" = 1
				decline

mission "A2 Republic Civic Capacity Practice Recovery"
	minor
	source
		government "Republic"
	destination "Earth"
	"offer precedence" 8
	to offer
		has "A2 Republic Civic Capacity Practice: decided"
		not "A2 Republic Civic Capacity Practice: recovery seen"
		"world: republic civic strain" <= 1
	on offer
		conversation
			branch continuity
				has "A2 Republic Civic Capacity Practice: continuity"
			branch capacity
				has "A2 Republic Civic Capacity Practice: capacity"
			branch exceptions
				has "A2 Republic Civic Capacity Practice: exceptions"
			`Ren remembers that you refused a standing rule. The queues are shorter now, and the temporary measures are being retired rather than quietly becoming permanent procedure.`
				goto finish
			label continuity
			`With the acute strain eased, Ren shows you a transferred housing case whose original intake date survived three office moves. The queue is shorter, but the important change is that recovery did not erase the person's accumulated wait.`
				goto finish
			label capacity
			`The public queue board now shows both delay and available staff. One office has a longer line but enough capacity to clear it; another has fewer cases and almost no trained reviewers. Routing follows the real constraint instead of appearances.`
				goto finish
			label exceptions
			`Ren closes an emergency-priority register. Every accelerated case still carries its reason and review note, allowing ordinary processing to resume without pretending the exceptions never happened.`
			label finish
			action
				"A2 Republic Civic Capacity Practice: recovery seen" = 1
				decline
