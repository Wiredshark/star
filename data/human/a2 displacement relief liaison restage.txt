# A2 dynamic narrative: make the authoritative A1 Republic-displacement -> Free
# Worlds relief spillover legible to the player without taking ownership of either
# simulation. Individual displacement history remains distinct from aggregate load.


mission "A2 Displacement Relief Liaison: Cross-Border Briefing"
	minor
	name `Relief Liaison: Cross-Border Cases`
	description `Imani Vale asks how Free Worlds relief staff should document arrivals connected to severe Republic displacement.`
	source
		government "Free Worlds"
		not attributes "station"
	"offer precedence" 9
	to offer
		has "world: republic displacement relief spillover active"
		"world: republic displacement pressure" >= 4
		not "A2 Displacement Relief Liaison: briefing seen"
	on offer
		conversation
			`Imani Vale catches you beside an arrivals board whose newest entries carry Republic departure records alongside Free Worlds relief numbers. "This is no longer only a local queue. Some of these cases are arriving because displacement pressure across the border is spilling into our network."`
			`She keeps her voice low. "I do not want a Republic crisis turned into a Free Worlds talking point, and I do not want the origin of these cases erased either. How should we record the connection?"`
			choice
				`\tRecord the cross-border cause, but keep individual arrivals anonymous.`
					goto bounded
				`\tTrack only aggregate capacity pressure; do not attach a political origin to individual cases.`
					goto aggregate
				`\tKeep the two systems operationally separate unless an arrival asks for the connection to be recorded.`
					goto consent
				`\tI am not authorizing a cross-border documentation policy.`
					goto refuse
			label bounded
			`Vale nods. "Cause without exposure. We can preserve the fact that displacement is moving demand across the border without turning a passenger list into evidence."`
			action
				"A2 Displacement Relief Liaison: briefing seen" = 1
				"A2 Displacement Relief Liaison: bounded provenance" = 1
				"A2 Displacement Relief Liaison: followup pending" = 1
				decline
			label aggregate
			`Vale marks the capacity ledger instead. "Then the shared fact is load, not identity. Ports can plan around the pressure without pretending every arrival has the same story."`
			action
				"A2 Displacement Relief Liaison: briefing seen" = 1
				"A2 Displacement Relief Liaison: aggregate only" = 1
				"A2 Displacement Relief Liaison: followup pending" = 1
				decline
			label consent
			`Vale closes the origin column. "Case by case, then. The network can coordinate capacity while the people moving through it decide whether their history travels with the file."`
			action
				"A2 Displacement Relief Liaison: briefing seen" = 1
				"A2 Displacement Relief Liaison: consent led" = 1
				"A2 Displacement Relief Liaison: followup pending" = 1
				decline
			label refuse
			`Vale locks the policy field. "Understood. I will record that you declined rather than treating silence as permission."`
			action
				"A2 Displacement Relief Liaison: briefing seen" = 1
				"A2 Displacement Relief Liaison: refused" = 1
				"A2 Displacement Relief Liaison: followup pending" = 1
				decline


mission "A2 Displacement Relief Liaison: Review"
	minor
	name `Relief Liaison: After the Crossing`
	description `Imani Vale reviews how the cross-border documentation rule behaved after the immediate spillover latch ended.`
	source
		government "Free Worlds"
		not attributes "station"
	"offer precedence" 9
	to offer
		has "A2 Displacement Relief Liaison: followup pending"
		not "world: republic displacement relief spillover active"
		not "A2 Displacement Relief Liaison: followup seen"
	on offer
		conversation
			branch bounded_high
				has "A2 Displacement Relief Liaison: bounded provenance"
				"world: republic displacement pressure" >= 4
			branch bounded_low
				has "A2 Displacement Relief Liaison: bounded provenance"
				"world: republic displacement pressure" < 4
			branch aggregate_high
				has "A2 Displacement Relief Liaison: aggregate only"
				"world: republic displacement pressure" >= 4
			branch aggregate_low
				has "A2 Displacement Relief Liaison: aggregate only"
				"world: republic displacement pressure" < 4
			branch consent_high
				has "A2 Displacement Relief Liaison: consent led"
				"world: republic displacement pressure" >= 4
			branch consent_low
				has "A2 Displacement Relief Liaison: consent led"
				"world: republic displacement pressure" < 4
			branch refused
				has "A2 Displacement Relief Liaison: refused"
			label bounded_high
			`"The first pulse passed, but Republic displacement is still severe," Vale says. "Anonymous provenance gave planners warning that another cross-border load may follow without exposing the people already here."`
			action
				"A2 Displacement Relief Liaison: bounded pressure persists" = 1
				goto finish
			label bounded_low
			`"Republic displacement has eased below the severe threshold," Vale says. "The anonymous provenance record now reads as history instead of an active warning, which is exactly the distinction we needed."`
			action
				"A2 Displacement Relief Liaison: bounded pressure eased" = 1
				goto finish
			label aggregate_high
			`"The crossing pulse ended, but the upstream pressure has not," Vale says. "Aggregate load tracking kept our ports ready without making assumptions about why any particular traveler left."`
			action
				"A2 Displacement Relief Liaison: aggregate pressure persists" = 1
				goto finish
			label aggregate_low
			`"Both the immediate pulse and the severe upstream pressure have eased," Vale says. "The capacity ledger was enough to scale back cleanly without leaving political labels attached to case files."`
			action
				"A2 Displacement Relief Liaison: aggregate pressure eased" = 1
				goto finish
			label consent_high
			`"The upstream pressure is still severe," Vale says. "Consent-led records are slower to summarize, but nobody had their displacement history carried forward simply because the network wanted cleaner statistics."`
			action
				"A2 Displacement Relief Liaison: consent pressure persists" = 1
				goto finish
			label consent_low
			`"The severe upstream pressure has eased," Vale says. "The consent-led files stayed attached only where people wanted them attached. We can close the emergency review without rewriting anyone's history for them."`
			action
				"A2 Displacement Relief Liaison: consent pressure eased" = 1
				goto finish
			label refused
			`Vale finds you after the immediate crossing pulse has ended. "I kept your refusal exactly where you left it. Nobody converted it into a documentation policy, and nobody treated silence as authorization."`
			action
				"A2 Displacement Relief Liaison: refusal respected" = 1
				goto finish
			label finish
			action
				"A2 Displacement Relief Liaison: followup pending" = 0
				"A2 Displacement Relief Liaison: followup seen" = 1
				decline
