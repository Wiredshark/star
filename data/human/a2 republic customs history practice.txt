mission "A2 Republic Customs History Practice"
	minor
	source
		government "Republic"
		not attributes "station"
	"offer precedence" 8
	to offer
		has "A2 Republic Customs Review: later reader seen"
		not "A2 Republic Customs History Practice: decided"
	on offer
		conversation
			`A customs archivist recognizes the review record you helped produce. She does not ask you to reopen it. Instead, she points to older Republic exhibits: amended manifests, written review bases, challenge ledgers, and waiting periods between repeated inspections.`
			`\t"Those practices survived because they separate different questions," she says. "Where did a claim come from? What actually triggered review? What was confirmed? What remains inference? And when does an old decision stop being a sufficient reason for a new one?"`
			`\tShe asks what lesson, if any, you would carry into future disputes. The answer is not a Republic credential or a rule for customs officers; it is your own working practice.`
			choice
				`Keep provenance and amendments visible whenever a record changes.`
					goto provenance
				`Separate review triggers, confirmed facts, inferences, and unresolved questions.`
					goto basis
				`Require a current reason before repeating an old review.`
					goto interval
				`Keep this case local instead of turning it into a standing practice.`
					goto local
			label provenance
			`You decide that a corrected record should retain the path by which it became corrected. The archivist calls that useful precisely because later information should not retroactively erase earlier uncertainty.`
			action
				"A2 Republic Customs History Practice: decided" = 1
				"A2 Republic Customs History Practice: provenance" = 1
				"A2 Republic Customs History Practice: reflection pending" = 1
				decline
			label basis
			`You decide to keep triggers, evidence, inference, and open questions distinct. The archivist notes that this can justify serious scrutiny when evidence supports it while making unsupported escalation easier to detect.`
			action
				"A2 Republic Customs History Practice: decided" = 1
				"A2 Republic Customs History Practice: basis separation" = 1
				"A2 Republic Customs History Practice: reflection pending" = 1
				decline
			label interval
			`You decide that repeating a review should require a current reason rather than the mere fact that a review happened before. The archivist stresses that fresh evidence still counts; repetition itself does not become evidence.`
			action
				"A2 Republic Customs History Practice: decided" = 1
				"A2 Republic Customs History Practice: current reason" = 1
				"A2 Republic Customs History Practice: reflection pending" = 1
				decline
			label local
			`You keep the customs history as context for this case rather than a portable rule. The archivist accepts the boundary without treating refusal to generalize as refusal to remember.`
			action
				"A2 Republic Customs History Practice: decided" = 1
				"A2 Republic Customs History Practice: local only" = 1
				"A2 Republic Customs History Practice: reflection pending" = 1
				decline

mission "A2 Republic Customs History Practice Reflection"
	minor
	source
		government "Republic"
		not attributes "station"
	"offer precedence" 8
	to offer
		has "A2 Republic Customs Review: later reader seen"
		has "A2 Republic Customs History Practice: reflection pending"
		not "A2 Republic Customs History Practice: reflection seen"
	on offer
		conversation
			branch provenance
				has "A2 Republic Customs History Practice: provenance"
			branch basis
				has "A2 Republic Customs History Practice: basis separation"
			branch interval
				has "A2 Republic Customs History Practice: current reason"
			`At another Republic port, a clerk recognizes that your earlier lesson was deliberately local. She uses the old case only as context while rebuilding today's record from today's sources. The resemblance is noted, but no borrowed rule is allowed to substitute for current evidence.`
				goto finish
			label provenance
			`At another Republic port, a clerk traces a corrected manifest through every amendment instead of replacing the old entry. The final record shows what changed, who changed it, and which earlier uncertainty remained reasonable before the correction arrived.`
				goto finish
			label basis
			`At another Republic port, a review note is split into four sections: trigger, confirmed fact, inference, and unresolved question. A serious concern remains visible, but an inherited suspicion can no longer masquerade as a confirmed finding simply because it appears in the same file.`
				goto finish
			label interval
			`At another Republic port, an examiner notices that an old review flag is being copied forward without a fresh basis. The historical flag stays searchable, but the new inspection is not opened until the office states what current fact actually justifies repeating it.`
			label finish
			`The result is mundane rather than triumphant. The value of the practice is that records remain inspectable and decisions revisable without pretending that procedure itself guarantees correctness.`
			action
				"A2 Republic Customs History Practice: reflection pending" = 0
				"A2 Republic Customs History Practice: reflection seen" = 1
				decline
