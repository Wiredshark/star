# A2 consumes the A1 Free Worlds repair backlog read-only. The player's priority is
# remembered across recovery, then interpreted against whether a patrol surge is
# still active when yard capacity has recovered.


mission "A2 Free Worlds Repair Priority: Yard Briefing"
	minor
	name `Free Worlds Yards: Repair Priority`
	description `A Free Worlds yard coordinator asks how a strained repair queue should allocate scarce dock time.`
	source
		government "Free Worlds"
		not attributes "station"
	"offer precedence" 9
	to offer
		"world: free worlds repair backlog" >= 3
		not "A2 Free Worlds Repair Priority: briefing seen"
	on offer
		conversation
			`Yard coordinator Mara Venn meets you beside a board of delayed maintenance slots. "The queue is no longer just inconvenient. Every berth we give one hull is a berth another crew waits for."`
			branch mobilized
				has "world: free worlds patrol surge"
			`Venn taps three priority rules. "What should survive the backlog: flight safety, patrol readiness, or predictable civilian turnaround?"`
				goto choose
			label mobilized
			`A patrol surge has filled the board with additional hulls. Venn grimaces. "Mobilization makes every priority look urgent at once. That is exactly when an unwritten rule becomes favoritism."`
			label choose
			choice
				`\tProtect flight-critical safety first, even if patrol and commercial schedules slip.`
					goto safety
				`\tProtect patrol readiness first while mobilization is stressing the yards.`
					goto patrol
				`\tProtect predictable civilian turnaround so freight and passenger operators can plan around the shortage.`
					goto civilian
				`\tI am not setting a standing repair priority for Free Worlds yards.`
					goto refuse
			label safety
			`Venn marks the rule plainly: airworthy before punctual. "Then nobody gets to disguise schedule pressure as a safety judgment."`
			action
				"A2 Free Worlds Repair Priority: briefing seen" = 1
				"A2 Free Worlds Repair Priority: priority safety" = 1
				decline
			label patrol
			`Venn marks mobilized defense hulls for the first available qualifying slots. "Temporary readiness priority, recorded as such. We will still know what civilian work it displaced."`
			action
				"A2 Free Worlds Repair Priority: briefing seen" = 1
				"A2 Free Worlds Repair Priority: priority patrol" = 1
				decline
			label civilian
			`Venn reserves a predictable share of the board for civilian operators. "Not fastest for everyone, but legible. A merchant can plan around a known delay better than a queue that changes every watch."`
			action
				"A2 Free Worlds Repair Priority: briefing seen" = 1
				"A2 Free Worlds Repair Priority: priority civilian" = 1
				decline
			label refuse
			`Venn closes the proposal. "Understood. Your refusal is not permission for me to write your name under somebody else's rule."`
			action
				"A2 Free Worlds Repair Priority: briefing seen" = 1
				"A2 Free Worlds Repair Priority: refused" = 1
				decline


mission "A2 Free Worlds Repair Priority: Recovery Review"
	minor
	name `Free Worlds Yards: After the Queue`
	description `With the repair backlog largely cleared, Mara Venn reviews what the earlier priority actually meant.`
	source
		government "Free Worlds"
		not attributes "station"
	"offer precedence" 9
	to offer
		has "A2 Free Worlds Repair Priority: briefing seen"
		"world: free worlds repair backlog" <= 1
		not "A2 Free Worlds Repair Priority: recovery seen"
	on offer
		conversation
			branch safety_surge
				has "A2 Free Worlds Repair Priority: priority safety"
				has "world: free worlds patrol surge"
			branch safety_quiet
				has "A2 Free Worlds Repair Priority: priority safety"
			branch patrol_surge
				has "A2 Free Worlds Repair Priority: priority patrol"
				has "world: free worlds patrol surge"
			branch patrol_quiet
				has "A2 Free Worlds Repair Priority: priority patrol"
			branch civilian_surge
				has "A2 Free Worlds Repair Priority: priority civilian"
				has "world: free worlds patrol surge"
			branch civilian_quiet
				has "A2 Free Worlds Repair Priority: priority civilian"
			`Venn shows you a blank policy field. "You declined to set the rule. The queue cleared without anybody converting that refusal into your endorsement."`
			action
				"A2 Free Worlds Repair Priority: refusal respected" = 1
				goto finish
			label safety_surge
			`"The backlog is down even though mobilization continues," Venn says. "Safety-first kept damaged hulls from being rushed back out, but patrol schedules absorbed most of the uncertainty."`
			action
				"A2 Free Worlds Repair Priority: Venn remembers safety under surge" = 1
				goto finish
			label safety_quiet
			`"With mobilization quiet, safety-first mostly changed documentation," Venn says. "Crews could point to a technical threshold instead of arguing whose schedule mattered more."`
			action
				"A2 Free Worlds Repair Priority: Venn remembers safety after quiet" = 1
				goto finish
			label patrol_surge
			`Venn points to a small reserve of open patrol slots. "The readiness rule still has a reason to exist while mobilization continues. The useful part is that its displaced civilian work stayed visible instead of vanishing from the ledger."`
			action
				"A2 Free Worlds Repair Priority: Venn remembers patrol under surge" = 1
				goto finish
			label patrol_quiet
			`"The surge ended before the rule could become habit," Venn says. "We retired the patrol preference with the condition that justified it instead of letting emergency priority become permanent seniority."`
			action
				"A2 Free Worlds Repair Priority: Venn remembers patrol after quiet" = 1
				goto finish
			label civilian_surge
			`"Mobilization is still active, but civilian operators kept a predictable share of the board," Venn says. "That cost some patrol flexibility, and at least the tradeoff is measurable rather than hidden in queue changes."`
			action
				"A2 Free Worlds Repair Priority: Venn remembers civilian under surge" = 1
				goto finish
			label civilian_quiet
			`"Once mobilization eased, the civilian reservation stopped being a scarce carve-out and became a scheduling discipline," Venn says. "Operators knew when to arrive, and empty berths stopped appearing between emergencies."`
			action
				"A2 Free Worlds Repair Priority: Venn remembers civilian after quiet" = 1
			label finish
			action
				"A2 Free Worlds Repair Priority: recovery seen" = 1
				decline
