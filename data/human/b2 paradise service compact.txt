mission "B2 Paradise Service Compact: Review"
	minor
	name `Who Keeps the Gate Open`
	description `Return after the service district has tested the transit arrangement you helped shape.`
	source
		government "Republic"
		attributes "paradise"
		not attributes "station"
	to offer
		has "B2 Paradise Service Compact: introduced"
		not "B2 Paradise Service Compact: reviewed"
	on offer
		conversation
			branch mercer
				has "B2 Paradise Service Compact: route mercer"
			branch voss
				has "B2 Paradise Service Compact: route voss"
			`Mercer and Voss have replaced their argument with a spreadsheet. The midnight shuttle is still running, but the municipality now pays for a defined late-service window while the estate keeps responsibility for the gate itself. "It is less satisfying than winning," Mercer says. Voss replies, "Which is usually how durable agreements feel."`
			choice
				`\tFormalize the shared-cost corridor as a standing service compact.`
					goto shared
				`\tTransfer the late route into the municipal network and compensate the estate for access.`
					goto public
			label mercer
			`The late shuttle has become busier. Mercer says, "Protecting access worked. Paying for it did not solve itself." Voss shows you a maintenance ledger. "We kept the gate open, but every delay is now treated as proof that the estate should absorb another public cost."`
			choice
				`\tMake the municipality responsible for the late route and access payment.`
					goto public
				`\tKeep estate operation but bind both sides to a shared-cost service compact.`
					goto shared
			label voss
			`The estate retained control of the gate, but worker complaints forced a temporary late-access guarantee. Mercer says, "We proved the route matters by nearly losing it." Voss replies, "And we proved that guarantees without funding are just invoices sent to whoever cannot close the road."`
			choice
				`\tTurn the late route into municipal transit with compensated estate access.`
					goto public
				`\tKeep the gate private but create a binding shared-cost service compact.`
					goto shared
			label public
			`You recommend treating the late service as ordinary public transit: the municipality owns the schedule and compensates the estate for defined access and security costs. Voss insists on measurable service terms. Mercer agrees to publish them. The old service road remains privately built, but the route across it is no longer privately discretionary.`
			action
				"B2 Paradise Service Compact: reviewed" = 1
				"B2 Paradise Service Compact: settlement municipal corridor" = 1
				"B2 Paradise Service Compact: mercer trusts player" = 1
			accept
			label shared
			`You recommend a standing compact: the estate keeps operating the gate, the municipality funds the late public-service window, and both sides publish a review schedule instead of treating every dispute as a referendum on ownership. Mercer calls it administratively ugly. Voss calls that a compliment.`
			action
				"B2 Paradise Service Compact: reviewed" = 1
				"B2 Paradise Service Compact: settlement shared service compact" = 1
				"B2 Paradise Service Compact: voss trusts player" = 1
			accept
