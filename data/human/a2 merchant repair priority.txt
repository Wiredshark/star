# A2 consumes the A1 Merchant repair backlog and surge read-only. A player-selected
# yard policy persists through recovery and is later interpreted against whether the
# A1 pooled-capacity surge is still active.


mission "A2 Merchant Repair Priority: Yard Briefing"
	minor
	name `Merchant Yards: Repair Priority`
	description `A Merchant yard coordinator asks how a strained repair queue should allocate scarce dock capacity.`
	source
		government "Merchant"
		not attributes "station"
	"offer precedence" 9
	to offer
		"world: merchant repair backlog" >= 3
		not "A2 Merchant Repair Priority: briefing seen"
	on offer
		conversation
			`Yard coordinator Elian Cross meets you beside a queue board crowded with rescue-damaged hulls. "Tugs got them home. That does not mean the yards can return all of them to service at once."`
			branch pooled
				has "world: merchant repair surge"
			`Cross lays out three rules. "What should scarce berth time protect first: safe return to service, continuity for working freight hulls, or the oldest repair obligations?"`
				goto choose
			label pooled
			`Temporary pooled capacity has opened extra berths, but Cross shakes his head. "A surge buys time. It does not answer who should get that time first."`
			label choose
			choice
				`\tProtect flight-critical safety first, regardless of schedule or contract age.`
					goto safety
				`\tProtect working freight continuity so active routes lose as little capacity as possible.`
					goto freight
				`\tProtect queue fairness: honor the oldest documented repair obligations first.`
					goto queue
				`\tI am not setting a standing priority for Merchant yards.`
					goto refuse
			label safety
			`Cross marks the rule plainly. "Then technical release criteria outrank commercial pressure. If a hull is unsafe, its place in the schedule cannot make it safe."`
			action
				"A2 Merchant Repair Priority: briefing seen" = 1
				"A2 Merchant Repair Priority: priority safety" = 1
				decline
			label freight
			`Cross reserves qualifying slots for ships already carrying active route obligations. "We will record what gets displaced. Continuity is a tradeoff, not a claim that freight matters more than every other crew."`
			action
				"A2 Merchant Repair Priority: briefing seen" = 1
				"A2 Merchant Repair Priority: priority freight" = 1
				decline
			label queue
			`Cross sorts the board by documented intake time. "Then exceptions have to be written as exceptions. Nobody quietly jumps the queue because their contract is louder."`
			action
				"A2 Merchant Repair Priority: briefing seen" = 1
				"A2 Merchant Repair Priority: priority queue" = 1
				decline
			label refuse
			`Cross closes the policy field. "Understood. Your refusal is not a blank signature for somebody else's yard rule."`
			action
				"A2 Merchant Repair Priority: briefing seen" = 1
				"A2 Merchant Repair Priority: refused" = 1
				decline


mission "A2 Merchant Repair Priority: Recovery Review"
	minor
	name `Merchant Yards: After the Queue`
	description `With the Merchant repair backlog largely cleared, Elian Cross reviews what the earlier priority actually changed.`
	source
		government "Merchant"
		not attributes "station"
	"offer precedence" 9
	to offer
		has "A2 Merchant Repair Priority: briefing seen"
		"world: merchant repair backlog" <= 1
		not "A2 Merchant Repair Priority: recovery seen"
	on offer
		conversation
			branch safety_surge
				has "A2 Merchant Repair Priority: priority safety"
				has "world: merchant repair surge"
			branch safety_quiet
				has "A2 Merchant Repair Priority: priority safety"
			branch freight_surge
				has "A2 Merchant Repair Priority: priority freight"
				has "world: merchant repair surge"
			branch freight_quiet
				has "A2 Merchant Repair Priority: priority freight"
			branch queue_surge
				has "A2 Merchant Repair Priority: priority queue"
				has "world: merchant repair surge"
			branch queue_quiet
				has "A2 Merchant Repair Priority: priority queue"
			`Cross shows you the cleared policy field. "You declined to set the rule. The queue recovered without anybody converting that refusal into your endorsement."`
			action
				"A2 Merchant Repair Priority: refusal respected" = 1
				goto finish
			label safety_surge
			`"Pooled capacity is still active," Cross says. "Safety-first kept marginal hulls from being rushed through the extra berths. The surge increased throughput without lowering the release threshold."`
			action
				"A2 Merchant Repair Priority: Cross remembers safety under surge" = 1
				goto finish
			label safety_quiet
			`"The temporary pool has closed," Cross says. "Safety-first outlasted it because the useful part was the release threshold, not the emergency capacity."`
			action
				"A2 Merchant Repair Priority: Cross remembers safety after quiet" = 1
				goto finish
			label freight_surge
			`Cross points to the remaining pooled berths. "Freight continuity used the surge aggressively. Routes recovered quickly, and the displaced non-route work stayed visible on the ledger instead of disappearing."`
			action
				"A2 Merchant Repair Priority: Cross remembers freight under surge" = 1
				goto finish
			label freight_quiet
			`"Once pooled capacity ended, freight preference stopped with the shortage that justified it," Cross says. "Working routes kept continuity without becoming permanent seniority over every later repair."`
			action
				"A2 Merchant Repair Priority: Cross remembers freight after quiet" = 1
				goto finish
			label queue_surge
			`"The surge gave us room to honor the old queue faster," Cross says. "Extra capacity did not become a second hidden priority list; documented intake order still explained who moved first."`
			action
				"A2 Merchant Repair Priority: Cross remembers queue under surge" = 1
				goto finish
			label queue_quiet
			`"With the surge gone, the oldest-obligation rule became ordinary queue discipline," Cross says. "Exceptions are still possible, but now they leave a reason somebody else can inspect."`
			action
				"A2 Merchant Repair Priority: Cross remembers queue after quiet" = 1
			label finish
			action
				"A2 Merchant Repair Priority: recovery seen" = 1
				decline
