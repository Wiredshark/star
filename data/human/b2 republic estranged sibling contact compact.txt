# B2 Republic Estranged Sibling Contact Compact.
# A civic-strain intake desk reconnects two adult siblings after years apart. The
# conflict is whether a truthful family link silently grants contact access or
# reconciliation authority. This is one family's resolution, not Republic law.


event "B2 Republic Estranged Sibling Contact Compact: Review Ready"
	"B2 Republic Estranged Sibling Contact Compact: review ready" = 1


mission "B2 Republic Estranged Sibling Contact Compact: Offer"
	minor
	name `A Name on the Intake Form`
	description `Two estranged adult siblings ask whether a verified family link should also reveal current contact information or imply willingness to reconcile.`
	source
		government "Republic"
		not attributes "uninhabited"
	"offer precedence" 8
	to offer
		has "world: republic civic strain" >= 2
		not "B2 Republic Estranged Sibling Contact Compact: introduced"
		not "B2 Republic Estranged Sibling Contact Compact: declined"
	on offer
		conversation
			`Mara Pell is helping at an overloaded civic intake desk when a family-link search finds her brother, Joren Pell. They have not spoken in eight years. The match is real; the system also exposes Joren's current berth and contact channel.`
			`Mara says, "I wanted to know he was alive. I did not ask for his address." Joren, reached through the desk, answers, "And I did not ask a database to decide that being siblings means I am ready to talk."`
			choice
				`\tKeep the verified family link, but require each adult's current consent before sharing direct contact information.`
					goto consent
				`\tAllow a neutral relay: either sibling may send a message without learning the other's current location or channel unless the recipient chooses to reply.`
					goto relay
				`\tKeep paired records: family relationship/history separate from current contact permission and reconciliation status.`
					goto paired
				`\tRefuse to turn one family's estrangement into a general rule.`
					goto decline
			label consent
			`You preserve the family match as history while treating current contact access as a separate permission. Mara can know the match was verified without receiving Joren's present location.`
			action
				"B2 Republic Estranged Sibling Contact Compact: introduced" = 1
				"B2 Republic Estranged Sibling Contact Compact: route current consent" = 1
				"B2 Republic Estranged Sibling Contact Compact: Mara trusts player" = 1
				event "B2 Republic Estranged Sibling Contact Compact: Review Ready" 7 11
				decline
			label relay
			`You recommend a one-way relay. A message can cross the gap without turning the intake office into an involuntary address book.`
			action
				"B2 Republic Estranged Sibling Contact Compact: introduced" = 1
				"B2 Republic Estranged Sibling Contact Compact: route neutral relay" = 1
				"B2 Republic Estranged Sibling Contact Compact: Joren trusts player" = 1
				event "B2 Republic Estranged Sibling Contact Compact: Review Ready" 7 11
				decline
			label paired
			`You separate what is historically true from what is currently permitted: sibling relationship, known-safe status, contact channel, permission to disclose it, and willingness to reconcile become linked but distinct facts.`
			action
				"B2 Republic Estranged Sibling Contact Compact: introduced" = 1
				"B2 Republic Estranged Sibling Contact Compact: route paired records" = 1
				"B2 Republic Estranged Sibling Contact Compact: Mara trusts player" = 1
				"B2 Republic Estranged Sibling Contact Compact: Joren trusts player" = 1
				event "B2 Republic Estranged Sibling Contact Compact: Review Ready" 7 11
				decline
			label decline
			`You tell them the desk can correct this case without making you the author of a wider family-contact rule.`
			action
				"B2 Republic Estranged Sibling Contact Compact: declined" = 1
				decline


mission "B2 Republic Estranged Sibling Contact Compact: Review"
	minor
	name `Contact Is Not Reconciliation`
	description `A copied family-link record now treats a neutral contact relay as proof that the siblings reconciled.`
	source
		government "Republic"
		not attributes "uninhabited"
	"offer precedence" 8
	to offer
		has "B2 Republic Estranged Sibling Contact Compact: introduced"
		has "B2 Republic Estranged Sibling Contact Compact: review ready"
		has "world: republic civic strain" <= 1
		not "B2 Republic Estranged Sibling Contact Compact: reviewed"
	on offer
		conversation
			branch relay
				has "B2 Republic Estranged Sibling Contact Compact: route neutral relay"
			branch paired
				has "B2 Republic Estranged Sibling Contact Compact: route paired records"
			`The crisis queue has eased, but a copied family record now says Mara and Joren are "reconnected." One message was delivered. Neither sibling agreed that the relationship itself was repaired.`
				goto settle
			label relay
			`The relay worked exactly once. A downstream clerk saw successful delivery and replaced "contact possible" with "family reconciled." Joren objects before the label spreads further.`
				goto settle
			label paired
			`The paired records survived until one export flattened relationship history and current contact state into a single household profile.`
			label settle
			choice
				`\tUse a portable family-contact packet: relationship history, current disclosure permission, relay state, direct-contact consent, reconciliation status, corrections, and closure travel together.`
					goto packet
				`\tUse fresh-contact renewal: a successful message or prior permission expires as authority for future disclosure unless each adult renews it.`
					goto renewal
			label packet
			`You make the distinctions travel with every copy. A reader can know the siblings are related without claiming they share an address, an open channel, or a repaired relationship.`
			action
				"B2 Republic Estranged Sibling Contact Compact: reviewed" = 1
				"B2 Republic Estranged Sibling Contact Compact: settlement portable contact packet" = 1
				"B2 Republic Estranged Sibling Contact Compact: Mara trusts player" = 1
				"B2 Republic Estranged Sibling Contact Compact: Joren trusts player" = 1
				decline
			label renewal
			`You make contact permission perishable. Family history remains true, but every new disclosure or direct channel requires a current decision from the living adults involved.`
			action
				"B2 Republic Estranged Sibling Contact Compact: reviewed" = 1
				"B2 Republic Estranged Sibling Contact Compact: settlement fresh contact renewal" = 1
				"B2 Republic Estranged Sibling Contact Compact: Joren trusts player" = 1
				decline


mission "B2 Republic Estranged Sibling Contact Compact: Mara Remembers"
	minor
	name `Mara Remembers`
	description `Mara remembers whether family history stopped being treated as permanent contact permission or proof of reconciliation.`
	source
		government "Republic"
		not attributes "uninhabited"
	"offer precedence" 8
	to offer
		not "B2 Republic Estranged Sibling Contact Compact: aftermath seen"
		or
			has "B2 Republic Estranged Sibling Contact Compact: settlement portable contact packet"
			has "B2 Republic Estranged Sibling Contact Compact: settlement fresh contact renewal"
	on offer
		conversation
			branch renewal
				has "B2 Republic Estranged Sibling Contact Compact: settlement fresh contact renewal"
			`Mara tells you that she and Joren have exchanged two messages since the review. "We are talking a little," she says. "That is true. Nobody else gets to promote 'a little' into a whole relationship for us."`
				goto finish
			label renewal
			`Mara says Joren recently allowed a new message through the relay, then let that permission expire. "The record finally understands that being my brother is permanent history. Access to him is not."`
			label finish
			action
				"B2 Republic Estranged Sibling Contact Compact: aftermath seen" = 1
				decline
