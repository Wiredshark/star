# A2 Republic Border Testimony Practice.
# Consumes B2 border-testimony aftermath and A1 border pressure read-only.

mission "A2 Republic Border Testimony Practice: Briefing"
	minor
	name `What Counts Tomorrow`
	description `Talia Rook asks what evidence discipline should survive the last border-alert review.`
	source
		government "Republic"
	"offer precedence" 8
	to offer
		has "B2 Republic Border Testimony Compact: aftermath seen"
		"world: republic border pressure" <= 2
		not "A2 Republic Border Testimony Practice: decided"
		not "A2 Republic Border Testimony Practice: refused"
	on offer
		conversation
			`Talia Rook has closed the last review, but not the question behind it. "The compact fixed one chain of records. What do you want people to remember when the next alert arrives?"`
			choice
				`\tPreserve source lineage: every operational claim should retain whether it is direct, derived, corrected, contradicted, open, or resolved.`
					goto lineage
				`\tPreserve independence: repeated copies may preserve history, but only genuinely independent observations increase corroboration.`
					goto independence
				`\tPreserve closure: resolved warnings remain searchable history but need genuinely new evidence before becoming active again.`
					goto closure
				`\tKeep the compact local. Do not turn one border-record settlement into a standing personal doctrine.`
					goto local
				`\tRefuse to adopt a standing evidence practice.`
					goto refuse
			label lineage
			`You choose lineage. Rook notes that a conclusion without its source history can become more confident each time it is copied.`
			action
				"A2 Republic Border Testimony Practice: decided" = 1
				"A2 Republic Border Testimony Practice: lineage" = 1
			accept
			label independence
			`You choose independence. Rook records that ten summaries of one patrol sighting remain one underlying observation, not ten witnesses.`
			action
				"A2 Republic Border Testimony Practice: decided" = 1
				"A2 Republic Border Testimony Practice: independence" = 1
			accept
			label closure
			`You choose closure. Rook records that searchable history should not behave like an active accusation after its evidence has been resolved.`
			action
				"A2 Republic Border Testimony Practice: decided" = 1
				"A2 Republic Border Testimony Practice: closure" = 1
			accept
			label local
			`You keep the lesson local. The compact remains useful context, not borrowed authority for every later dispute.`
			action
				"A2 Republic Border Testimony Practice: decided" = 1
				"A2 Republic Border Testimony Practice: local" = 1
			accept
			label refuse
			`You refuse to establish a standing practice. Rook records the boundary and does not schedule a later review.`
			action
				"A2 Republic Border Testimony Practice: refused" = 1
			decline


mission "A2 Republic Border Testimony Practice: Recurrence"
	minor
	name `The Next Alert`
	description `A later Republic border alert tests the evidence practice you chose after the testimony compact.`
	source
		government "Republic"
	"offer precedence" 8
	to offer
		has "A2 Republic Border Testimony Practice: decided"
		"world: republic border pressure" >= 4
		not "A2 Republic Border Testimony Practice: recurrence seen"
	on offer
		conversation
			branch independence
				has "A2 Republic Border Testimony Practice: independence"
			branch closure
				has "A2 Republic Border Testimony Practice: closure"
			branch local
				has "A2 Republic Border Testimony Practice: local"
			`The next alert produces a familiar stack of reports. Rook points out that several alarming summaries still trace back to one patrol observation. Because source lineage survived, the operations desk can see the real evidentiary shape before deciding what the alert means.`
			goto record
			label independence
			`The next alert arrives with six reports, but four derive from the same witness statement. Your independence rule keeps the copied material visible without allowing repetition to manufacture corroboration. Two genuinely separate observations remain two observations.`
			goto record
			label closure
			`A historical warning resurfaces during the next alert. Its old disposition travels with it, so clerks do not silently reactivate it. A new patrol observation is recorded separately and can be judged on its own evidence.`
			goto record
			label local
			`The next alert reaches a different port with different records and personnel. Rook uses the old compact as context, not authority. The local office documents its own evidence chain instead of pretending your earlier settlement automatically governs this case.`
			label record
			action
				"A2 Republic Border Testimony Practice: recurrence seen" = 1
			accept
