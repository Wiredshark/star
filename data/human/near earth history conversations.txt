		not attributes "station"
	"offer precedence" 5
	to offer
		random < 1
	on offer
		conversation
			`A long wall near the spaceport concourse is covered by a map of human settlement routes. The oldest paths cluster tightly around Sol before spreading outward in layers, each generation of routes drawn in a slightly different color. Small plaques beside the map describe migrations not as a single outward march, but as repeated cycles of departure, return, consolidation, and departure again.`
			`	A teacher nearby reminds a group of students that many Near Earth ports became important not because they were final destinations, but because people learned to treat them as stepping stones: places to repair ships, gather information, find work, and decide whether the next jump was worth the risk.`

	to offer
		random < 1
	on offer
		conversation
			`Behind glass in a public lobby sits a stack of reproduced maintenance ledgers from an early orbital service cooperative. Most entries are mundane: replacement seals, patched radiators, borrowed tools, fuel transferred between ships, and labor traded against future work.`
			`	A note beside the display argues that Near Earth infrastructure was built as much through these repetitive obligations as through famous expeditions. Reliable travel depended on crews knowing that a damaged ship could find compatible parts, trained mechanics, and someone willing to keep a promise made several ports earlier.`
