mission "A2 Rook Mediation: First Meeting"
	minor
	name `Rook Mediation`
	description `Port mediator Imani Rook asks how a disputed convoy-loss claim should be investigated.`
	source "New Boston"
	"offer precedence" 9
	to offer
		not "A2 Rook Mediation: briefing seen"
	on offer
		conversation
			`Imani Rook, a port mediator with a slate full of damage reports, stops you near the New Boston concourse. "Captain <last>, I need an outside view. A freight captain and a yard crew are blaming each other for a convoy loss, and both sides have enough evidence to make this ugly."`
			`Rook turns the slate so you can see three conflicting timelines. "I am not asking you to decide who deserves to win. I am asking what kind of evidence should control what happens next."`
			choice
				`\tHear both accounts, preserve the uncertainty, and reconcile the timelines before assigning blame.`
					goto balanced
				`\t[Combat experience: convoy command] Reconstruct the loss from formation discipline, threat contacts, and escort decisions.`
					to activate
						"combat rating" >= 5
					goto command
				`\t[Prior service: Deep convoy] Compare manifests, rendezvous timing, and escort obligations before hearing closing arguments.`
					to display
						has "Deep: Syndicate Convoy: done"
					goto logistics
				`\tDecline to let either side use your name as outside authority.`
					goto refuse
			label balanced
			`You ask Rook to separate testimony from records and preserve both parties' claims until the timelines can be reconciled. She nods. "That buys us time without pretending uncertainty is proof."`
			action
				"A2 Rook Mediation: briefing seen" = 1
				"A2 Rook Mediation: route balanced" = 1
				"A2 Rook Mediation: review pending" = 1
				decline
			label command
			`You point out that formation spacing, reaction times, and hostile contacts can distinguish equipment failure from command failure without guessing at motive. Rook marks the route on her slate. "Good. Experience tells us which evidence to test; it does not get to replace the evidence."`
			action
				"A2 Rook Mediation: briefing seen" = 1
				"A2 Rook Mediation: route command" = 1
				"A2 Rook Mediation: review pending" = 1
				decline
			label logistics
			`You recognize the same handoff ambiguity that can sink a convoy contract before the first jump. You tell Rook to compare manifests, departure acknowledgements, and escort assumptions. "A prior service record is not authority by itself," she says, "but it can tell us which documents matter."`
			action
				"A2 Rook Mediation: briefing seen" = 1
				"A2 Rook Mediation: route logistics" = 1
				"A2 Rook Mediation: review pending" = 1
				decline
			label refuse
			`You tell Rook that a neutral signature would only disguise uncertainty as judgment. She closes the slate. "Fair. Refusal is better than false certainty. I will record that you declined rather than letting either side turn silence into support."`
			action
				"A2 Rook Mediation: briefing seen" = 1
				"A2 Rook Mediation: refused" = 1
				"A2 Rook Mediation: refusal reader pending" = 1
				decline

			action

				goto finish
			label command
			`Rook shows you a revised incident map. "The formation reconstruction held up. 			action
				"A2 Rook Mediation: outcome command" = 1
				goto finish
			label logistics
			`Rook lays two manifests side by side. "The handoff records exposed the failure. 			action
				"A2 Rook Mediation: outcome logistics" = 1
			label finish
			`She closes the case file. "The settlement preserves the disputed evidence and the corrected timeline. More importantly, neither side gets to rewrite what we did into a claim that experience made somebody automatically right."`

				"A2 Rook Mediation: review pending" = 0
