# A2 Drak Stewardship Echo.
# Consumes the resolved B2 memorial-custody compact without asserting Drak command
# authority, bureaucracy, or a public Drak communications system.

mission "A2 Drak Stewardship Echo: Offer"
	entering
	minor
	invisible
	source
		system "Sayaiban"
	to offer
		has "B2 Drak Memorial Custody Compact: aftermath seen"
		not "A2 Drak Stewardship Echo: introduced"
	on offer
		conversation
			branch archive
				has "B2 Drak Memorial Custody Compact: settlement severed function archive"
			`The familiar Drak presence brushes your thoughts again. The Custodian remains only your private name for its cadence. This time there is no vanished machine before you. Instead, you see a younger species uncovering a dangerous relic of its own past and arguing over whether safety requires forgetting what the relic once did.`
			`\tThe bounded memorial you helped describe appears only as a pattern: an inaccessible original, surrounded by reconstructions whose omissions and substitutions remain visible. The younger observers can learn from what was contained without mistaking a safe copy for untouched history.`
			goto choose
			label archive
			`The familiar Drak presence brushes your thoughts again. The Custodian remains only your private name for its cadence. This time there is no vanished machine before you. Instead, you see a younger species uncovering a dangerous relic of its own past and arguing over whether safety requires forgetting what the relic once did.`
			`\tThe severed-function archive you helped describe appears only as a pattern: a harmless working copy beside an immutable record of the dangerous function and the intervention that disabled it. The younger observers can use the safe system without pretending that safety was part of the original design.`
			label choose
			`\tYou understand the question without words. Should this precedent remain a private lesson between you and the Custodian, or may its principle be carried into later encounters where it could help others reason about dangerous inheritance?`
			choice
				`\tKeep the precedent private. A lesson shown in confidence should not become authority over strangers.`
					goto private
				`\tCarry the principle forward, but only as advice with its origin and limits made explicit.`
					goto bounded
				`\tCarry only the method: separate evidence, intervention, and present-day operation without invoking the Drak as authority.`
					goto method
			label private
			`You hold the memory close. The Custodian's attention recedes without disappointment. Whatever value the precedent has, you will not spend another being's trust to make your argument stronger.`
			action
				"A2 Drak Stewardship Echo: introduced" = 1
				"A2 Drak Stewardship Echo: precedent private" = 1
			accept
			label bounded
			`You accept the principle as something you may repeat only with its boundaries attached: it came from one problem, under one form of custody, and it does not make you a Drak representative. The Custodian leaves you with the image of a record whose margins remain attached to every copy.`
			action
				"A2 Drak Stewardship Echo: introduced" = 1
				"A2 Drak Stewardship Echo: precedent bounded advice" = 1
			accept
			label method
			`You keep no claim of endorsement. What you carry forward is a procedure: distinguish the original evidence from later intervention, distinguish both from what is safe to operate now, and make those boundaries visible. The Custodian answers with three separate patterns that never collapse into one.`
			action
				"A2 Drak Stewardship Echo: introduced" = 1
				"A2 Drak Stewardship Echo: precedent method only" = 1
			accept

mission "A2 Drak Stewardship Echo: Later Reflection"
	entering
	minor
	invisible
	source
		system "Fasitopfar"
	to offer
		has "A2 Drak Stewardship Echo: introduced"
		not "A2 Drak Stewardship Echo: reflection seen"
	on offer
		conversation
			branch private
				has "A2 Drak Stewardship Echo: precedent private"
			branch method
				has "A2 Drak Stewardship Echo: precedent method only"
			`The Custodian returns only long enough to show you a later dispute. Someone has repeated the stewardship principle as advice, but has also repeated its limits: no Drak mandate, no universal rule, no claim that one settlement answers every dangerous inheritance. The people hearing it argue with the principle instead of submitting to its source. That, you realize, was the boundary you chose.`
			goto done
			label private
			`The Custodian returns only long enough to show you another people reaching a similar distinction on their own. They preserve evidence, mark intervention, and separate safe operation from historical truth without ever hearing the precedent you kept private. The resemblance is imperfect, which makes it more convincing: restraint did not prevent others from reasoning for themselves.`
			goto done
			label method
			`The Custodian returns only long enough to show you a technical review where three columns are kept separate: original evidence, later intervention, and present operation. Nobody invokes the Drak. Nobody invokes you. The method survives because it can be examined on its own merits rather than borrowed authority.`
			label done
			action
				"A2 Drak Stewardship Echo: reflection seen" = 1
			accept
