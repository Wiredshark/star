# A2 Rulei Exposure Practice. Downstream player-facing practice built from the
# resolved B2 exposure-accountability arc. It does not establish Rulei causation,
# motive, medical authority, or representative authority.

mission "A2 Rulei Exposure Practice: Offer"
	minor
	name `What the Warning Carries`
	description `Orlov asks what lesson should survive when a bounded Rulei-contact record is reused outside the case that produced it.`
	source "Earth"
	"offer precedence" 9
	to offer
		has "B2 Rulei Exposure Accountability: aftermath seen"
		not "A2 Rulei Exposure Practice: introduced"
	on offer
		conversation
			branch escrow
				has "B2 Rulei Exposure Accountability: settlement consent escrow"
			`Dr. Sena Orlov shows you a training copy of the bounded exposure certificate. The important part, she says, is not the Rulei-contact label. It is that observation, current fitness, follow-up, and the date on which a warning loses authority travel together. Eli Verran adds that the certificate still says nothing about what caused the symptoms or what the Rulei intended.`
				goto choose
			label escrow
			`Dr. Sena Orlov shows you a training request built from the consent-escrow settlement. Purpose, audience, permitted fields, and expiry are visible before any record opens. Eli Verran points out that testimony remains separately controlled and that neither a copied warning nor an analyst's interpretation becomes a diagnosis merely by traveling farther.`
			label choose
			choice
				`\tCarry forward a bounded-warning practice: whenever a risk marker travels, its evidence, present status, and removal or review condition should travel with it.`
					goto bounded
				`\tCarry forward a consent-and-purpose practice: secondary use should name why the record is needed, who receives it, and when that permission ends.`
					goto consent
				`\tKeep this lesson local to Rulei-contact records rather than treating one unusual case as a universal policy.`
					goto local
				`\tDecline to adopt a standing practice from someone else's medical and testimony dispute.`
					goto refuse
			label bounded
			`You keep the narrow lesson: a warning without its evidence and exit condition is not the same record. Orlov approves of the emphasis on current findings. Verran approves of the expiration boundary. Neither treats the practice as proof of Rulei causation.`
			action
				"A2 Rulei Exposure Practice: introduced" = 1
				"A2 Rulei Exposure Practice: bounded warning" = 1
				decline
			label consent
			`You keep the permission lesson instead. A record may be useful in a new setting, but that new use should identify its purpose, audience, and expiry rather than silently inheriting every old permission. Orlov notes that treatment facts can still move when needed. Verran notes that interpretation still requires its own authority.`
			action
				"A2 Rulei Exposure Practice: introduced" = 1
				"A2 Rulei Exposure Practice: consent purpose" = 1
				decline
			label local
			`You decide the case is too unusual to become a general rule. Orlov records that the exposure system remains useful on its own terms. Verran is satisfied that an uncertain contact history has not quietly become a template for judging unrelated people.`
			action
				"A2 Rulei Exposure Practice: introduced" = 1
				"A2 Rulei Exposure Practice: local only" = 1
				decline
			label refuse
			`You decline to turn the dispute into a standing practice. Orlov and Verran leave the existing settlement intact without attaching your name to a broader lesson.`
			action
				"A2 Rulei Exposure Practice: introduced" = 1
				"A2 Rulei Exposure Practice: refused" = 1
				decline

mission "A2 Rulei Exposure Practice: Reflection"
	minor
	name `A Warning With an Exit`
	description `A later administrative request tests what you chose to remember from the Rulei exposure dispute.`
	source "Earth"
	"offer precedence" 9
	to offer
		has "B2 Rulei Exposure Accountability: aftermath seen"
		has "A2 Rulei Exposure Practice: introduced"
		not "A2 Rulei Exposure Practice: refused"
		not "A2 Rulei Exposure Practice: reflection seen"
	on offer
		conversation
			branch bounded
				has "A2 Rulei Exposure Practice: bounded warning"
			branch consent
				has "A2 Rulei Exposure Practice: consent purpose"
			branch local
				has "A2 Rulei Exposure Practice: local only"
			`The record has no valid persisted practice route. Orlov closes the request rather than inventing one.`
				goto finish
			label bounded
			`Orlov shows you an unrelated occupational warning whose copied version omitted the review date. Remembering the practice you chose, you ask for the current finding and the condition that removes the warning before anyone treats it as permanent. The correction is procedural, not a claim that the Rulei case explains this one.`
				goto finish
			label consent
			`A research office asks to reuse an old exposure summary for a purpose never named in the original request. Remembering the practice you chose, you ask them to state the audience, fields, purpose, and expiry before access changes. Verran notes that the question is about permission, not whether the old interpretation was true.`
				goto finish
			label local
			`A clerk suggests importing the Rulei exposure template into an unrelated personnel review. You point out that the original settlement answered a specific evidence-and-consent problem under unusual uncertainty. The clerk uses the local review rules instead. Keeping a precedent bounded proves useful too.`
			label finish
			action
				"A2 Rulei Exposure Practice: reflection seen" = 1
				decline
