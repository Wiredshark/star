# reinterpretations as ordinary cultural life for ages. Human settlement in Hai
# space adds another layer: artists and collaborators can move between cultural
# expectations without making a stage persona equivalent to a private identity.

mission "B2 Hai Stage Persona Compact: Review"
	minor
	name `When the Profile Becomes a Biography`
	description `Return after Kessa and Mara test whether a stage persona can survive copying without turning performance into private biography.`
	source
		government "Hai"
		not attributes "uninhabited"
	"offer precedence" 8
	to offer
		has "language: Hai"
		has "B2 Hai Stage Persona Compact: introduced"
		has "B2 Hai Stage Persona Compact: review ready"
		not "B2 Hai Stage Persona Compact: reviewed"
	on offer
		conversation
			branch bounded
				has "B2 Hai Stage Persona Compact: route bounded performance"
			branch revision
				has "B2 Hai Stage Persona Compact: route revision authority"
			`Mara shows you three copied profiles from unrelated venues. One preserves the campaign slogan but drops its expiry. Another credits the label for a joke Kessa rewrote herself. A third cites both copies and calls the resulting repetition independent evidence that Kessa is personally reckless. Kessa says the most irritating part is that none of the copies is entirely fabricated; they are simply no longer saying what their sources meant.`
				goto settle
			label bounded
			`The label marked the original profile as a tour persona, but a foreign entertainment feed copied only the colorful claims and omitted the campaign boundary. Kessa says a temporary performance became a permanent personality because the boring metadata did not travel with the entertaining part.`
				goto settle
			label revision
			`Kessa revised the persona for a new release, but several promoters kept using the older profile because it was already translated and familiar. Mara says revision authority means little if downstream copies can erase the revision date and present an obsolete version as current.`
			label settle
			choice
				`\tCreate a portable persona packet: campaign role, credited authors, approved claims, consent scope, revision history, expiry, and private-biography exclusions travel together.`
					goto packet
				`\tCreate a persona firewall: promotional claims expire unless renewed, while private biographical claims require a separate attributable source rather than inference from performance.`
					goto firewall
			label packet
			`You recommend that the useful metadata become inseparable from the appealing copy. Every profile carries which campaign it belongs to, who created and revised the stage character, what Kessa actually approved for publicity, when those approvals expire, and which claims are explicitly performance rather than private biography. Repetition of the same source remains one lineage, not independent corroboration.`
			action
				"B2 Hai Stage Persona Compact: reviewed" = 1
				"B2 Hai Stage Persona Compact: settlement portable persona packet" = 1
				"B2 Hai Stage Persona Compact: kessa trusts player" = 1
				"B2 Hai Stage Persona Compact: mara trusts player" = 1
				decline
			label firewall
			`You recommend a harder boundary. Promotional persona claims are current only for the campaign that authorizes them and expire unless deliberately renewed. Anyone making a private biographical claim must identify a separate source instead of reasoning backward from a costume, lyric, staged rivalry, or old publicity line. Kessa says fans can still play along; they simply stop being drafted as accidental biographers.`
			action
				"B2 Hai Stage Persona Compact: reviewed" = 1
				"B2 Hai Stage Persona Compact: settlement persona firewall" = 1
				"B2 Hai Stage Persona Compact: kessa trusts player" = 1
				decline
