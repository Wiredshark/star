mission "A2 Heliarch Procedure Reflection"
	minor
	source
		government "Coalition"
	"offer precedence" 4
	to offer
		random < 1
		has "license: Coalition"
		not "A2 Heliarch Procedure Reflection: resolved"
	on offer
		conversation
			`You pause at a Heliarch archive terminal after reading several institutional records. The exhibits have a common theme: written procedure can preserve disagreement, evidence, and responsibility across a large institution. They are equally clear that a procedure is evidence of how a decision was reached, not proof that the decision was correct.`
			`\tA public annotation prompt asks what lesson you intend to carry into your own dealings with Coalition institutions. It does not ask you to speak for the Heliarchs.`
			choice
				`Treat procedure as a record that makes authority inspectable.`
					goto inspectable
				`Treat procedure as a starting point that must remain open to contrary evidence.`
					goto revisable
				`Keep the archives as context, not as a rule for future disputes.`
					goto contextual
			label inspectable
				`You save a private note: when authority matters, ask what was recorded, who objected, who was responsible, and whether the chain can later be inspected.`
				accept
			label revisable
				`You save a private note: procedure is useful when it preserves enough evidence to expose its own mistakes and permit revision.`
				accept
			label contextual
				`You save no general rule. The records remain historical context rather than borrowed Heliarch authority.`
				accept
	on accept
		conversation
			`The terminal closes without endorsement or credential. Whatever lesson you took from the archive remains your own.`
		decline
