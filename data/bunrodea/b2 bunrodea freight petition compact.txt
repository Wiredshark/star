	name `The Missing Seal`
	description `Megasa freight coordinator Sedi Var and Erabu estate factor Iral Kes disagree over how a disputed cargo record should travel through Bunrodea petition review.`

		government "Bunrodea"
	to offer
		not "B2 Bunrodea Freight Petition Compact: introduced"
		not "B2 Bunrodea Freight Petition Compact: declined"
	on offer
		conversation
			`A freight office near the spaceport keeps copies of old cargo seals beside modern manifests. Two Bunrodea stand over a shipment record whose final page bears a bright red rejection mark. Sedi Var, a Megasa freight coordinator, explains through a translation terminal that their convoy delivered the machinery exactly as the common manifest required. Iral Kes, an Erabu estate factor, answers that the receiving estate refuses liability because one aristocratic countersignature is missing.`
			`Sedi taps the standardized destination code. "The port knew what arrived, when it arrived, and which crew carried it. The missing seal changes a petition, not the cargo." Iral folds his forearms. "And a petition without the recognized chain of authority becomes a rumor with numbers attached. A clerk cannot decide that an estate's signature has ceased to matter."`

				`\tBack Sedi: operational facts should keep cargo moving while the ownership petition is reviewed separately.`

				`\tBack Iral: a disputed shipment needs a complete petition chain before liability transfers.`
					goto iral
				`\tPropose a paired docket that separates verified freight facts from the unresolved estate claim.`
					goto paired
				`\tDecline to referee Bunrodea freight procedure.`
					goto decline
			label sedi
			`You argue that the common freight register exists precisely so a vessel, dock, or workshop does not have to renegotiate every basic fact. Delivery time, seal condition, route, quantity, and destination can be accepted as operational facts while an estate's liability petition continues through review. Sedi's antennae lift. Iral replies, "Then the record must state exactly which facts the port accepted, or temporary practice will quietly become judgment."`

			accept
			label iral
			`You tell Sedi that a standardized manifest cannot by itself decide who has assumed an estate obligation. If liability transfers before the petition chain is complete, later clerks may inherit a decision that nobody actually made. Iral inclines his head. Sedi answers, "Then the petition chain needs a clock. A missing seal cannot freeze a dock forever."`
			action
				"B2 Bunrodea Freight Petition Compact: introduced" = 1
				"B2 Bunrodea Freight Petition Compact: route iral" = 1
				"B2 Bunrodea Freight Petition Compact: iral trusts player" = 1

The port can certify transport facts in a common freight record while a linked petition docket carries ownership, liability, missing signatures, and later rulings. Iral starts drafting the cross-reference that would bind those facts to the unresolved petition.`

mission "B2 Bunrodea Freight Petition Compact: Review"
	minor
	name `Facts That Survive Appeal`
	description `Return after Sedi Var and Iral Kes have tested the freight-and-petition procedure you helped shape.`
	source
		government "Bunrodea"
	to offer
		has "B2 Bunrodea Freight Petition Compact: introduced"
		not "B2 Bunrodea Freight Petition Compact: reviewed"
	on offer
		conversation
			branch sedi
				has "B2 Bunrodea Freight Petition Compact: route sedi"
			branch iral
				has "B2 Bunrodea Freight Petition Compact: route iral"
			`Sedi and Iral have turned one corner of the freight office into a wall of paired records. Each shipment has a compact transport sheet linked to a petition docket. Sedi points to a case that crossed three ports without repeating a measurement or cargo inspection. Iral points to the same case and notes that the receiving estate's liability remained explicitly unresolved until a clerk accepted the missing signature explanation.`
			choice
				`\tFormalize a portable petition docket: verified freight facts become fixed entries, while liability and ownership rulings remain separately appealable.`
					goto portable
				`\tKeep a dual ledger: one operational freight record and one authority record, linked by permanent cross-references and deadlines.`
					goto dual
			label sedi
			`Sedi shows you a shipment that was unloaded on time because the port accepted the common manifest, even though an Erabu estate later disputed who should pay for damage discovered in storage. The operational record kept moving; the petition did not disappear. Iral admits that the separation worked, but only because every accepted freight fact was copied into the petition docket before the cargo left the port.`
			choice
				`\tMake those locked freight facts the foundation of a portable petition docket.`
					goto portable
				`\tKeep two linked ledgers so operational acceptance never looks like a liability ruling.`
					goto dual
			label iral
			`Iral shows you a petition that remained open until the final estate countersignature arrived. The cargo was not reassigned in the meantime, but Sedi's crew had to keep answering the same questions at every transfer point because the petition did not expose which freight facts were already settled. Sedi says, "Authority was preserved. Memory was not." Iral does not disagree.`
			choice
				`\tCarry settled freight facts forward inside a portable petition docket.`
					goto portable
				`\tUse two linked ledgers with explicit deadlines so neither side has to infer what the other already accepted.`
					goto dual
			label portable
			`You recommend a portable petition docket. Freight facts certified at the port of transfer become fixed entries: quantity, destination, seal condition, time, route, and custodian. Ownership and liability remain appealable fields with their own signatures and rulings. Sedi calls it a record that lets a Megasa crew finish one job before an Erabu household finishes arguing about another. Iral calls it an appeal that can no longer rewrite the evidence it began from.`
			action
				"B2 Bunrodea Freight Petition Compact: reviewed" = 1
				"B2 Bunrodea Freight Petition Compact: settlement portable docket" = 1
				"B2 Bunrodea Freight Petition Compact: sedi trusts player" = 1
			accept
			label dual
			`You recommend preserving two records. The freight ledger contains operational facts and transfer status; the petition ledger contains authority, liability, missing approvals, deadlines, and rulings. Permanent cross-references bind them together, and a petition that misses its review deadline must say why. Iral accepts that no estate signature has been demoted into a cargo field. Sedi accepts that no missing signature can make completed freight work vanish from the record.`
			action
				"B2 Bunrodea Freight Petition Compact: reviewed" = 1
				"B2 Bunrodea Freight Petition Compact: settlement dual ledger" = 1
				"B2 Bunrodea Freight Petition Compact: iral trusts player" = 1
			accept
