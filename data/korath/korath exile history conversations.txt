			`A Remnant archive has assembled old reports of Korath exile raids into a single ledger. The entries are not arranged by battle, but by what disappeared afterward: preserved food, reactor parts, medicine, tools, replacement hull material, and occasionally whole cargo loads that seem mundane beside the weapons carried by the raiders.`
			`\tAn archivist explains that the pattern changed how the Remnant interpreted the exile threat. The Korath near the galactic core are dangerous raiders, but their targets also reveal chronic shortages. A raid can be both an act of violence and evidence that a fleet or settlement lacks the supply network needed to obtain ordinary necessities peacefully.`
			`\tThe ledger therefore records losses separately from presumed motives. It is meant to preserve two facts at once: the raids harmed real communities, and the cargo taken can still reveal the material pressures shaping exile behavior.`

			`A tactical archive maps Korath exile fleets by recurring ship combinations, repair signatures, recovered cargo, and the systems where each group was observed. Many entries remain uncertain because damaged ships change hands, are rebuilt from salvaged components, or vanish into regions where the Remnant cannot follow them safely.`
			`\tA defense specialist says the record exists because a hostile fleet is not only a collection of hulls. Its history includes where it refuels, which components it can replace, what cargo it steals, which routes it revisits, and how quickly losses are repaired. Those patterns reveal whether a threat is an isolated raiding group or part of a larger support network.`
			`\tThe archive deliberately avoids assigning every Korath ship to a single command structure. The exiles are treated as a population with overlapping fleets and material dependencies, not as a perfectly understood military organization.`
				decline


mission "Remnant History: Korath Recovery and Containment Ledger"
	minor
	invisible
	source
		government "Remnant"
	to offer
		has "Remnant: Cognizance 2: done"
		random < 4
	on offer
		conversation
			`A recovery ledger lists the aftermath of encounters with Korath exile ships: survivors found, wrecks inspected, unfamiliar components isolated, contaminated cargo discarded, and equipment transferred to researchers only after its origin and condition were recorded.`
			`\tA Remnant technician explains that repeated contact forced them to separate battlefield victory from what came afterward. A disabled ship can contain wounded enemies, useful evidence, hazardous technology, stolen civilian goods, or equipment whose effects are not understood. Treating all of it as ordinary salvage would erase information and create new risks.`
			`\tThe resulting procedure is deliberately conservative. Rescue, evidence preservation, technical study, and disposal are recorded as different obligations. The ledger has become part of how the Remnant remembers the exile conflict: not only as a sequence of attacks, but as an accumulating body of material evidence that must be handled without turning every discovery into another weapon.`
				decline
